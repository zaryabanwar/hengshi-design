## Detailed Records and Boundaries

### D-001 — Non-destructive repository recovery

Continue the existing repository without re-bootstrap, wholesale replacement,
destructive migration, or cleanup of user-owned work. Current code, documents,
seeds, and the exterior GLB are protected prototype evidence, not production
authority or launch proof.

### D-002 — Definition before application development

The complete product, SEO, identity, content/proof, UX/UI, 3D, architecture,
API/data, AI, security/privacy, QA, deployment, cost, growth, and compatibility
package must be independently reviewed and founder-approved before Phase 2 source
work begins.

### D-003 — Codex-native phased delivery

Codex remains the orchestrator; no separate workflow service/control plane will be
built. The Hengshi project manager uses bounded specialists and independent
reviewers. Material phases follow create, validate, inspect, review, revise,
validate again, founder approval. Default revisions are two and creative/3D
revisions are three.

### D-004 — Latest mutually compatible stable technology

Retain the approved technology families and select exact versions as a coupled
compatibility graph immediately before each upgrade wave. Use Context7 plus
official primary sources/registries, deterministic locks, migration and rollback
evidence. Preview, beta, RC, nightly, and experimental packages cannot become
production requirements. A blocked newest major needs an approved ADR and
quarterly recheck.

### D-005 — Audience, sectors, and conversion

Serve global English-speaking mid-market and enterprise technology, data, product,
transformation, and innovation buyers. Agriculture leads, mining follows, and
defense is separately gated. Sell strategy-to-delivery programs through custom
discovery; the primary CTA is a 30-minute booking qualified by verified email,
organization/role, outcome, service wing, timing, and consent. Budget is optional.

### D-006 — Brand and claims

Keep the public name Hengshi Design and create a complete premium future-ready
identity with an evidence-led, clear-expert voice and architectural mineral-neutral
visual direction. “World-first” is internal only unless independently substantiated
and legally approved. Unsupported superiority, performance, client, award, rating,
or outcome claims are prohibited.

### D-007 — Proof, experts, and AI-assisted content

Publish only verified client cases or clearly separated Hengshi-owned capability
demos. Quarantine fictional seeds. Named qualified specialists verify substantive
content and the founder gives final claim/publication approval. AI may assist but
cannot replace original expert value or evidence review; mass-generated pages are
prohibited.

### D-008 — SEO-first semantic publication

Every indexable URL returns complete unique semantic HTML before JavaScript and
without canvas. Quick Access provides equivalent content and conversion. The
canonical host is `https://hengshidesign.com`; URLs are lowercase, hyphenated,
stable, and use no trailing slash except root. `/world`, admin, preview, chat,
session, staging, filter, internal-search, draft, and rejected surfaces stay out of
indexes and sitemaps.

### D-009 — Routes and content operations

Launch canonical homepage, service/wing/room, agriculture/mining, verified work,
labeled demo, insight, expert, trust, about, contact, and booking routes. English is
the only launch language. The floor includes all service/sector pages and six
cornerstone expert-reviewed insights; later cadence is two expert pieces monthly
and one substantial research/tool asset quarterly after the workflow stabilizes.
No deceptive or paid-link authority schemes are allowed.

### D-010 — Campus information architecture

Use a central atrium with five wings and ten formal services: Strategy; Digital
Products/Growth; AI/Data/Automation; Immersive/Creative; Cloud/Reliability/Trust.
Shared spaces are verified Work, Industry, Trust, Insights, and Meeting. Concise
room signs pair with exact formal titles in semantic content and contracts.

### D-011 — Foundation and wing sequence

The foundation includes the complete responsive site, lightweight loader,
skippable drone arrival, entrance, reception, atrium, closed future wings, one
approved Reception AI Concierge, human handoff, and booking. Open specialist wings
in this order: AI/Data, Strategy, Immersive/Creative, Digital Products/Growth,
Cloud/Reliability/Trust.

### D-012 — Interaction and accessibility

Use directed point-and-click exploration, authored camera moves, and optional
free-look; no WASD, gamification, or easter eggs. Provide persistent directory,
skip/onboarding, local return preferences, optional post-interaction audio, WCAG
2.2 AA, keyboard/screen-reader parity, reduced motion, captions/transcripts,
low-power, asset-failure, and non-WebGL recovery.

### D-013 — 3D and asset provenance

Keep the current GLB untouched for comparison. Build a versioned project-owned
Blender master with corrected scale/origin, entrance, atrium, modular interiors,
materials, LODs, and web exports; switch only after side-by-side approval. Reuse
requires proven commercial modification rights, provenance, attribution, recorded
changes, and security review.

### D-014 — Azure-managed architecture

Retain a React/TypeScript/Vite/Tailwind/R3F frontend and modular FastAPI/SQLAlchemy
monolith. Add deterministic prerendering, Azure Front Door/static storage,
Container Apps, PostgreSQL, Azure Managed Redis, AI Search, Monitor/Application
Insights, and GitHub OIDC CI. PostgreSQL is authoritative; SQLite is explicit
test-only. Use development and production environments, immutable zero-traffic
candidates, manual promotion, and rollback.

### D-015 — AI providers and grounding

Use Azure and production-licensed NVIDIA adapters behind policy routing for task,
quality, cost, safety, classification, residency, and availability. Free NIM is
development-only. AI answers approved versioned evidence, exposes sources, says
“cannot verify” when needed, and cannot bind scope, price, timeline, legal terms,
or delivery. Azure handles approved PII/confidential EU traffic; NVIDIA initially
receives public/de-identified data only.

### D-016 — Human handoff and booking

Allow anonymous initial text chat; voice/video is opt-in. Explicit staff status
creates availability, while Teams presence only suppresses false availability.
Staff work in the Hengshi console with governed Teams alerts. Route direct bookings
to approved owner/backups through Outlook/Teams with visitor-local time, verified
email, idempotency, and reconciliation.

### D-017 — Privacy and sensitive data

No visitor account or upload at launch. Non-consented chat is ephemeral; consented
chat/brief/lead records expire after 90 days unless governed as an active-client or
legal record. Provide verified deletion including derived traces and restore
tombstones. Never train on visitor chat. Stop/warn/redact and route securely when
secrets, classified, or controlled defense data appears. Analytics are first-party,
cookieless, aggregate-only.

### D-018 — Identity and security

Staff use Entra SSO mapped to author, reviewer, founder approver, and administrator
roles plus two monitored dormant local JWT/bcrypt break-glass accounts with MFA,
randomly generated 24+ character credentials, controlled activation, alerts, and
post-use rotation. Browser sessions use rotating
HttpOnly/Secure/SameSite JWT cookies, CSRF, explicit logout, 30-minute idle and
eight-hour absolute timeouts. Require layered rate limits, least privilege,
CORS/CSP/WAF, redacted logs, encryption, prompt-injection controls, and independent
security review.

The 2026-07-19 Phase 1 requirements review exposed that the original durable
summary had omitted the already approved `24+` credential-length requirement.
This entry restores that founder-approved master-plan wording; it does not add a
new security decision.

### D-019 — Atomic publication

Use Draft, specialist evidence/SEO review, founder approval, render/index, public.
Authors cannot self-approve. Draft/rejected content never reaches public HTML,
sitemaps, search notifications, retrieval, or AI. A failed render/index keeps the
prior release active. One checksum ties HTML/metadata, structured data, redirects,
sitemaps, AI knowledge, and 3D panels.

### D-020 — Performance, reliability, and measurement

Target LCP <=2.5s, INP <=200ms, CLS <=0.1 at p75; initial 3D transfer <=8MB desktop
and <=4MB mobile, and each room <=5MB/2.5MB. Prove 25 concurrent visitors and three
chats. Phase 1 must propose measurable device-tier frame-rate, memory, and thermal
targets for founder approval rather than inheriting legacy FPS thresholds. Target
monthly 99.9%; retain RPO <=24h and RTO <=8h as an explicit reassessment risk.
Measure qualified organic discovery/bookings rather than traffic alone.

### D-021 — Supply-chain governance

Use weekly grouped dependency proposals, immediate security alerts, human-approved
minor/patch updates, never-auto-merged majors, per-release SBOM/license evidence,
monthly next-stable canaries, quarterly database/cloud checks, and no unresolved
production-critical deprecation. Triage critical exploitable vulnerabilities
immediately and target supported high-severity fixes within seven days.

### D-022 — Cost, assets, and external actions

envelope. Any other paid service/tool/asset needs separate approval with cost and a
free alternative. Free assets require commercial modification rights and audited
provenance. Credentials, paid activation, provisioning, DNS, migrations, commits,
pushes, PRs, and deployment retain explicit gates.
