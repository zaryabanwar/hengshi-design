# Accessibility, SEO, and Applications

## Accessibility specification

The identity targets WCAG 2.2 AA and requires independent accessibility review.
This producer package does not claim conformance.

### Non-negotiable behaviors

- Full name, category, formal service title, evidence label, limitation, status,
  and action exist as semantic text.
- Declared text pairings meet at least 4.5:1. Focus and meaningful non-text
  graphics meet at least 3:1; the recommended focus cyan on ink is 10.86:1.
- Zoom to 200% and reflow to 320 CSS px preserve reading and action order.
- Keyboard order follows document meaning; visible focus is never clipped or
  replaced by glow alone.
- Icons have text labels where meaning is not universal; SVG specimens include
  titles/descriptions but later UI controls still need accessible names.
- Status and data meaning use text plus shape/line/marker, not color alone.
- Reduced-motion, low-power, non-WebGL, asset-failure, forced-colors, and print
  expressions retain complete meaning and conversion.
- Audio is opt-in/off by default; meaningful media has captions/transcripts.
- Identity does not rely on texture, depth, transparency, blur, or material.

### Required independent checks

1. Automated contrast recomputation and manual adjacent-color review.
2. Mark/lockup inspection at 16, 20, 24, 32, rejected 144, supported 168 CSS px,
   and 7/38 mm print.
3. 400% zoom, 320 CSS px reflow, Windows High Contrast/forced colors, grayscale,
   and monochrome print.
4. Keyboard and screen-reader reading order on the static board.
5. Reduced-motion, no-animation, no-CSS-image, and unavailable-font behavior.
6. Color-vision simulation for status and data examples, confirming redundant
   encodings.

## Cultural and sector risk

| Risk | Identity treatment |
|---|---|
| Mineral/architectural expression feels austere, masculine-coded, colonial, or extractive | Warm stone neutrals, open fields, human-scale imagery, and comprehension testing; mineral is a structural metaphor, not sector imagery |
| Agriculture becomes pastoral, primitive, uniform, or rescue-oriented | No leaf logo or stock trope; reviewed context and real provenance only |
| Mining becomes machinery, danger, extraction, or scale spectacle | No pickaxe/mountain icon; center reviewed operational, human, environmental, data, reliability, and trust context |
| “Global” becomes borderless operating-footprint or cultural-uniformity claim | Full name and plain global English; intended audience only; no globe mark or location claim |
| AI becomes magic, sparkle, humanoid, or uncontrolled autonomy | Visible AI label, source/refusal/human route, structured node icon rather than sparkle |
| Defense leaks through command/mission/target language | No tactical shapes, shields, reticles, war-room language, or public defense application |
| Luminous color suggests hype or speculative technology | Accents remain narrow, semantic, labeled, and subordinate to mineral neutrals |

## SEO and entity identity

- Use **Hengshi Design** in the visible identity, title, metadata, credits, and
  approved Organization/WebSite representations. The monogram never becomes the
  entity name.
- The category and promise are approved strategy, but exact publication copy
  still follows content and founder publication review.
- A visual headline or canvas treatment never replaces one visible H1, complete
  initial HTML, landmarks, crawlable links, breadcrumbs, and truthful metadata.
- Formal service and Agriculture/Mining titles remain exact; decorative signage
  cannot rename them.
- `/world` remains a nonindexable optional presentation linked to canonical
  semantic routes.
- Organization logo structured data remains held until the final mark, rights,
  trademark, domain/entity facts, and publication release are approved.

## Social and Open Graph

The package includes an exploratory `og-evidence-in-motion.svg` composition and
local raster preview. They are not publishable social metadata.

Production social imagery must:

- use 1200 × 630 px as the primary composition, with safe central crop testing;
- show the full “Hengshi Design” name and truthful page subject;
- keep essential text away from approximately 8% edge-safe areas;
- avoid client logos, people, metrics, awards, offices, certifications, outcomes,
  and “live” capabilities without evidence and rights;
- use licensed/versioned assets with manifest, caption/alt decision, and checksum;
- match the visible page, title, description, canonical, and publication release.

## Favicon and app-icon implications

`favicon-framework-relay.svg` is the exploratory
`framework-relay-favicon-16-v1` whole-pixel optical variant for 16–23 CSS px
only. The standard master begins at 32 CSS px and the separate
`framework-relay-small-24-v1` covers 24–31 CSS px. The checkpoint is absorbed
into the final favicon step so a sub-pixel decorative detail is not introduced.
The final favicon family must be optically redrawn and tested at 16, 20, 24, 32,
48, 180, 192, and 512 px, in light/dark browser chrome and monochrome. A favicon
does not replace the full text entity name or establish trademark clearance.

## Print implications

- Use ink-on-paper or graphite-on-mist as the default. Signals convert to one
  solid ink plus line/marker pattern in monochrome.
- Do not assume RGB signal colors reproduce in CMYK, spot ink, office printers,
  emboss, foil, or uncoated stock. Physical proofs and production profiles are
  required later.
- Minimum print sizes and clearspace follow the logo specification.
- Evidence source, date, limitation, and link/identifier remain legible; do not
  hide them in pale footnotes.
- No metallic foil, specialty stock, or paid print process is approved here.

## Layout system

- Twelve-column wide grid, six-column medium grid, and four-column narrow grid;
  layouts collapse by content need rather than device prestige.
- Base spacing unit 4 px; primary rhythm 8/12/16/24/32/48/64/96 px.
- Decision content leads; evidence rail follows; action is adjacent and
  proportionate.
- Use asymmetry to create controlled motion, but maintain DOM/reading order.
- Cards are not the default container for every item; use open sections, rules,
  tables, and grouped lists to preserve enterprise scanability.
- Radius is restrained: 0–4 px for evidence frames, 8 px for controls/panels, pill
  shapes only for short status labels with visible text.

## Representative applications

### Semantic homepage hero

Text order: full name → category → promise → plain explanation → Quick Access and
optional immersive choice → booking action. The diagonal signal is decorative
unless its relationship is also explained in text. No proof wall appears while
the verified inventory is empty.

### Service decision unit

Formal service title, intended decision context, scope/limits, evidence state,
related Trust/Insight, and discovery action. The signal rail links sections but
does not imply a delivered result.

### Evidence card

Visible evidence type, owner/source, review date, limitation, and canonical link.
Verified Work and Hengshi-owned Demos use different label, shape, and line
patterns. Empty states remain candid.

### Trust topic

Separates requirement, implemented control, test evidence, limitation, owner,
scope, and review date. The identity cannot upgrade a target into assurance.

### Booking

Clear 30-minute discovery-conversation expectation, required/optional fields,
consent, email-verification state, booking-confirmation state, local time, and
recovery. No immersive, AI, audio, proof-review, or account dependency.

## Prohibited uses

- Public or production use before founder selection, independent review,
  trademark/legal clearance, and publication approval.
- Unsupported world-first, unique, leading, best, global-office, client, award,
  outcome, sector-history, security, compliance, or availability implication.
- Full-screen glow, dark-pattern scarcity, animated metrics, deceptive status,
  inaccessible low contrast, canvas-only copy, or hidden Quick Access.
- Client-style examples, unverified experts, synthetic social proof, or prototype
  seeds used to make the identity look populated.
