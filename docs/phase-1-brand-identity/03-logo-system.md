## Authoritative construction

`geometry-spec.json` is the single machine-readable logo authority. The standard
mark uses exact `framework-relay-master-80-v1` geometry:

- 80 × 80 viewBox;
- four open bracket paths at `M30 10 H10 V30`, `M50 10 H70 V30`,
  `M70 50 V70 H50`, and `M30 70 H10 V50`;
- bracket stroke 6 with square caps and miter joins;
- stepped relay `M16 52 H32 V40 H48 V28 H64`, stroke 5;
- square checkpoint x=61, y=25, width=6, height=6; and
- no diagonal, H-derived rail/bridge geometry, enclosing border, shield, badge,
  letter, gradient, glow, or implied verification tick.

The four brackets derive from Quiet Framework's governed modularity. The stepped
relay integrates the retained Signal Ledger system's structured trajectory in
discrete accountable movement. This reconciliation is more than the unchanged
Direction B exploration and deliberately does not reuse the rejected Signal
Ledger logo.

Primary, light, monochrome, reverse, and standard-mark SVGs use these exact
coordinates. The horizontal lockup viewBox is `0 0 420 128`; the master is
translated by 24,24; the wordmark begins at x=124 with a 34-unit font size and
baseline y=69. Category and promise remain separate semantic text, never embedded
in the logo.

Only two optical deviations are permitted:

1. `framework-relay-small-24-v1` for 24–31 CSS px aligns bracket returns,
   three-step relay, and checkpoint to a 24-unit grid with 2-unit strokes.
2. `framework-relay-favicon-16-v1` for 16–23 CSS px aligns all retained strokes
   to whole pixels and removes the separate checkpoint; the relay endpoint carries
   that function.

Any other geometry change requires a new authority, documented rationale,
validation, independent review, and founder decision. No producer revision
remains after iteration 3.

## Full-name-first rule

Use “Hengshi Design” in first contact, page/entity headings, metadata, accessible
names, structured data, legal contexts, credits, social identity, and anywhere the
mark could be ambiguous. The mark alone never defines the entity, service, state,
control, or evidence status. Icon-only controls require accessible names and
visible text wherever ambiguity remains.

## Clearspace

Define **x** as one 10-unit framework return in the standard master.

- Mark alone: minimum `2x` (20 master units) on every side.
- Horizontal lockup: `2x` outside; internal mark/name gap is 20 units in the
  lockup construction and must not be reduced.
- No rule, crop, image, evidence badge, status marker, motion path, or other
  bracket enters the clearspace.
- Do not repeat the logo brackets as a surrounding UI/card frame.

## Small-size behavior

- At 32 px and above, use `mark-framework-relay.svg` with exact master geometry.
- At 24–31 px, use only `mark-framework-relay-small.svg`.
- At 16–23 px, use only `favicon-framework-relay.svg`.
- Do not add the separate checkpoint to the 16-unit favicon or remove it from the
  24/80-unit forms.
- Do not round bracket corners, smooth the stepped relay into a diagonal, or
  enclose the open framework.
- Test on DPR1 and actual target devices before any production master is approved.

## Color, monochrome, and reverse

On ink, use paper brackets plus dark-surface cyan relay/checkpoint. On paper, use
ink brackets plus `#006D82` light-surface cyan. Color never changes geometry or
evidence meaning.

Monochrome uses one solid ink for brackets, relay, checkpoint, and name. Reverse
uses one white ink on black. The stepped path and open brackets must remain
distinguishable in office print, low-bandwidth documents, emboss/deboss, laser
etch, and single-ink fabrication; actual physical proofs remain later gates.

## Background rules

- Prefer flat ink, graphite, paper, mist, white, or black fields with declared
  contrast.
- On photography, use an opaque approved field; never use shadow or glow to rescue
  contrast.
- Do not place the logo over mineral texture, detailed imagery, data plots, or 3D
  reflections.
- Do not recolor brackets by service wing, sector, evidence state, or status.
- The square checkpoint is a logo element, not the Proposed evidence-state square.

## Prohibited uses

- No stretching, rotating, skewing, outlining, beveling, extruding, gradient,
  glow, continuous animation, crop, badge/shield enclosure, or corner rounding.
- No return to the superseded diagonal H-derived Signal Ledger logo.
- No use of the unchanged Quiet Framework source exploration as the current logo.
- No checkmark, certification seal, approval badge, product-platform glyph,
  agriculture leaf, mining mountain/pickaxe, defense/tactical symbol, globe, AI
  sparkle, circuit trace, client, location, award, or partner variant.
- No registration, filing, merchandise, public structured-data logo, shared
  library, or production use before the applicable human gates.
