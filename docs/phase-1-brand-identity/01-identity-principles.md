# Identity Principles

## Strategic translation

**Evidence in Motion** is not a decorative motion theme. It is a visible system
for showing how a consequential ambition becomes a decision frame, connected
capabilities, evidence, and a next accountable step. The identity must remain
understandable when all animation, imagery, sound, WebGL, and color are removed.

## Principles

### 1. Evidence has form

Evidence states use stable labels, line patterns, geometry, and text—not glow or
color alone. A verified fact, Hengshi-owned capability demonstration, proposed
method, requirement, limitation, and unavailable state must never share an
ambiguous badge treatment.

### 2. Motion reveals a relationship

The current Framework Relay mark uses four open governed brackets to define a
reviewable frame, a non-diagonal stepped evidence relay to show accountable
progression, and a square checkpoint to identify the current evidence point. In
layouts, motion may show connection, sequence, state change, or orientation. It
must not decorate an idle page or delay meaning.

### 3. Mineral calm, luminous precision

Ink, graphite, slate, stone, mist, and paper create architectural restraint.
Luminous cyan and amber are brief signals for action and attention. Lime and
violet are reserved data accents. Large luminous fields, rainbow gradients, and
ambient glow as a substitute for hierarchy are prohibited.

### 4. Semantics lead

The full name, category, formal service title, evidence label, and next action
must appear as text. A mark, room sign, icon, image, or spatial location cannot be
the only explanation. Quick Access is not a lower-quality brand mode.

### 5. Precision stays human

Strong grids, exact labels, and narrow signal lines are balanced by generous
space, warm stone neutrals, clear sentence case, and candid limitations. The
system avoids cold institutional density and luxury-coded ornament.

### 6. Craft earns attention

Distinctiveness comes from original proportion, disciplined negative space,
evidence choreography, and coherent application—not novelty claims or reference
resemblance. The identity does not imply awards, customers, global offices,
certifications, sector leadership, or measured outcomes.

### 7. Recovery is branded quality

Reduced-motion, monochrome, print, low-power, non-WebGL, asset-failure, keyboard,
and screen-reader experiences are authored outcomes. They preserve identity,
content, evidence state, and conversion rather than collapsing into a generic
fallback.

## Originality boundary

The approved synthesis of ScienceSoft, Lusion, Bruno Simon, and Active Theory
informs only the need to combine enterprise clarity, proof adjacency, authored
craft, and equal semantic access. This package copies none of their identity,
type, color, logo, motion, interaction, page composition, geometry, claims, or
content treatment. The current Framework Relay mark is an original geometric
construction made from four open governed brackets, one non-diagonal stepped
evidence relay, and one square checkpoint. The iteration-2 diagonal two-rail and
joining-field mark is founder-rejected, superseded, and preserved only in the
explicit archive as unresolved historical evidence.

## Identity conformance test

A later concept conforms only when all answers are yes:

1. Can a buyer identify Hengshi Design and the next useful action without seeing
   the mark or animation?
2. Does the system make proof type and limitation visible without relying on
   color, depth, glow, sound, or motion?
3. Does controlled energy remain subordinate to enterprise decision clarity?
4. Are Quick Access, reduced-motion, non-WebGL, monochrome, and print expressions
   complete rather than reduced?
5. Are agriculture and mining treated without pastoral, extractive, rescue, or
   machinery spectacle?
6. Are defense metaphors and unsupported superiority absent?
7. Can every included asset be traced to a local original source and explicit
   clearance state?
