# Imagery, Iconography, and Data Language

## Imagery posture

Imagery must clarify context, material, system, evidence, or human decision. It
must not manufacture proof, expertise, scale, location, infrastructure,
certification, customer history, or a future capability presented as current.

## Photography

### Preferred qualities

- documented human-scale work: review, mapping, prototyping, testing, and
  collaboration, with consent and provenance;
- close material and interface detail with real scale cues;
- restrained architectural light, warm neutrals, and controlled highlights;
- agriculture and mining contexts only after subject-matter review, rights, and
  evidence approval;
- captions that distinguish where, when, what, and whether the image is
  documentary, illustrative, or a controlled demonstration.

### Prohibited treatments

- unverified people posed as employees, clients, experts, or operators;
- stock handshake, globe/network, anonymous server-room, robot, or AI-brain
  clichés;
- pastoral agriculture stereotypes, rescue narratives, or uniform “farmer”
  imagery;
- mining machinery spectacle, extraction heroism, environmental erasure, or
  safety/compliance implication;
- military, command-center, target, weapon, war-room, or mission imagery;
- fake dashboards, invented metrics, fabricated case-study screens, or client
  logo composites;
- the protected exterior GLB or its geometry as a production identity asset.

## Illustration

Use sparse system diagrams, material sections, evidence flows, and decision maps.
Build with rails, nodes, brackets, numbered steps, and open fields. Every diagram
requires a title, textual explanation, reading order, labels, and a nonvisual
equivalent. A diagram may simplify a relationship but cannot upgrade evidence
status.

### AI-generated media

No generated media is included in this package. A later AI-media proposal must:

1. identify the generator, model/version where available, date, prompter/editor,
   source inputs, and modifications;
2. confirm approved commercial rights and record provider terms applicable at
   creation time;
3. avoid real-person likeness, client identity, protected style imitation,
   trademark-sensitive content, deceptive documentary treatment, or confidential
   input;
4. label the asset as illustrative or AI-assisted where context could mislead;
5. receive cultural, accessibility, evidence, security/privacy, rights, and
   founder review before publication.

Generated imagery can illustrate a proposed concept; it can never serve as
evidence that a place, person, customer, system, outcome, or delivery exists.

## Evidence labels on media

Every substantive image or video manifest entry must include:

- content identity and owner;
- creator and creation date;
- source/provenance and modification history;
- license/rights and clearance state;
- evidence class: documentary, approved client Work, Hengshi-owned demo,
  illustrative, or proposed concept;
- caption, alt decision, dimensions/duration, and crop/thumbnail variants;
- linked claim/source and approval state.

If any required field is missing, the media remains held.

## Iconography

### Construction

- 24 × 24 unit grid; 2-unit base stroke; square terminals with slight optical
  softening; no filled pictograms except status shapes.
- Primary icons combine one structural rail with one action or state cue.
- Minimum screen size 20 CSS px; use 24 CSS px for controls.
- Icons inherit current text color and remain visible in forced-colors mode.

### Meaning

| Concept | Shape language | Text requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Explore/connect | Two nodes joined by an open path | Visible action verb where not universally understood |
| Evidence/source | Stacked record plus source point | Evidence type and source/date |
| Quick Access | Open doorway plus direct horizontal path | `Quick Access` |
| Immersive view | Framed perspective field | `Immersive view` and equal Quick Access option |
| Booking | Calendar outline plus confirmed point | State-specific booking text |
| Human help | Person-neutral speech form | Availability state; never implied live |
| AI help | Labeled circuit node | Visible `AI` label; cannot impersonate a person |
| Reduced motion | Shortened path plus pause bars | `Reduced motion` preference/state |
| Audio | Speaker form | On/off state and transcript/caption route |

Do not assign wing or sector meaning through icons alone. Avoid generic sparkles
for AI, shields for unverified trust, leaves for agriculture, pickaxes/mountains
for mining, or tactical symbols for any public context.

## Data visualization

### Direct-labelled illustrative series

Illustrative chart series are separate from evidence state. The current
specimen names every series at its line endpoint and repeats the mapping in
semantic text:

| Series | Marker | Line pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Context baseline | plus | custom `1 5 9 5` |
| Dependency map | ring | custom `12 3 3 3 3 3` |
| Decision frame | cross | solid |
| Evidence depth | hexagon | `5 5` |
| Risk view | X | `13 4 4 4` |

These encodings must not be reused as evidence-state markers or described as an
evidence status. Direct labels remain visible at narrow widths, in grayscale,
and in forced colors; an accessible description or exact-value table repeats
the same names. This rule closes R-020 for the identity package.

### Series encoding

Use color plus a stable marker and line pattern:

| Series | Color | Marker | Line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cyan | Circle | Solid |
| 2 | Amber | Triangle | Long dash |
| 3 | Lime | Square | Short dash |
| 4 | Violet | Diamond | Dash-dot |
| 5 | Stone/ink contrast | Cross | Dotted |

Direct labels are preferred to legends where space permits. Tables remain
available for exact values. Charts require a title, unit, time period, sample or
scope, source, limitations, and a plain-language summary. Zero baselines must not
be truncated when doing so distorts comparison. No three-dimensional chart,
decorative area fill, animated count-up, or unsupported benchmark is permitted.

### Evidence and uncertainty

- Proposed targets use dashed outlines and the label `Target`.
- Measured values use solid marks only after source and method are approved.
- Missing values remain missing and labeled; do not interpolate visually without
  explicit method disclosure.
- Confidence/uncertainty uses a labeled interval plus pattern, not opacity alone.
- “Verified” is not a visual synonym for “positive.” It is an evidence status.
