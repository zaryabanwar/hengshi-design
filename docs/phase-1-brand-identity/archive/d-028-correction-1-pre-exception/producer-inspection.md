   Framework open modules and integrates Signal Ledger as a stepped, non-diagonal
   evidence relay with a square checkpoint. Shared square geometry, stroke logic,
   spacing, and topology make one mark rather than an unchanged B mark pasted into A.

   exploration, current candidate, superseded logo, and closed historical
   exploration. It contains no unresolved A/B/C selection gate.
3. **Logo family:** Primary, light, monochrome, reverse, standard 80-unit mark,
   24-unit optical mark, and 16-unit optical favicon match `geometry-spec.json`.
4. **Size behavior:** 32, 24, 20, and 16 CSS px marks remain recognizable. The
   168 CSS px lockup is supported; 144 CSS px is visibly and mathematically
   rejected. The 38 mm print minimum remains conditional on physical proof.
5. **Monochrome/modes:** Grayscale, forced-color, and print captures retain
   bracket/relay topology, semantic text, direct labels, and evidence-state
   shape/line redundancy.
6. **R-020 closure:** Context baseline, Dependency map, Decision frame, Evidence
   depth, and Risk view are visibly labelled at their endpoints and repeated in
   semantic HTML. Their marker/pattern grammar is distinct from the six evidence states.
7. **Content boundary:** The exact category and promise remain. No client,
   outcome, office, certification, expert, legal-entity, superiority, or production
   claim was introduced.
8. **Accessibility/SEO:** Full-name-first entity meaning, semantic initial HTML,
   Quick Access, non-WebGL equivalence, reduced motion, keyboard/forced-colors,
   canonical content, noindex review state, and Organization-logo hold remain.
9. **Licensing:** No third-party image, font file, generated image, paid asset,
   protected prototype asset, or external runtime dependency is included.
