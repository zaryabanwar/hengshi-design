## Current reconciled candidate — Framework Relay

**Geometry key:** `framework-relay-master-80-v1`

### Idea

Four open modular brackets define an accountable but unsealed framework. A
non-diagonal stepped relay crosses the field in discrete increments and ends at a
square evidence checkpoint. The frame expresses Quiet Framework's governed
modularity; the relay integrates Signal Ledger's structured trajectory and
Evidence in Motion without reusing the founder-rejected diagonal H-derived mark.

### Formal language

- **Logo:** four open corner brackets, three-step horizontal/vertical relay, one
  square checkpoint, and the full Hengshi Design name in lockups.
- **Layout:** retained Signal Ledger asymmetry and evidence rails; the logo does
  not force the whole identity into symmetrical grids.
- **Color:** retained ink/paper foundation. Cyan carries the active relay;
  monochrome/reverse collapse frame, relay, and checkpoint to one ink.
- **Typography:** unchanged system sans and monospaced evidence metadata.
- **Motion implication:** if later approved, reveal the relay one discrete step at
  a time and settle at the checkpoint; reduced motion shows the complete mark.
- **Entity/access:** the full “Hengshi Design” name leads first contact,
  metadata, structured data, legal/accessibility, and ambiguous contexts.

### Reconciliation test

The candidate is coherent only if all conditions remain true:

1. Removing the relay leaves recognizable Quiet Framework-derived open brackets.
2. Removing the brackets leaves a controlled stepwise trajectory, not the old
   diagonal Signal Ledger mark.
3. The combined form is reproducible from one geometry authority and survives
   dark, light, mono, reverse, 32, 24, and 16 CSS px behavior.
4. The logo remains secondary to the full name and exact semantic content.
5. The retained Signal Ledger system continues to govern layout, evidence,
   typography, imagery, motion, sound, 3D, accessibility, and SEO behavior.

### Tradeoffs

- The framework is calmer and more procurement-legible than the superseded mark,
  but requires open space so it does not become a badge or certification seal.
- The stepped relay is deliberately less cinematic than a diagonal gesture; the
  broader system carries energy through layout, signals, and purposeful motion.
- Repeated use of brackets could become institutional, so the mark is not a
  default container motif.
- The square checkpoint must not be interpreted as “verified”; evidence status
  is always expressed by the separate six-state grammar and visible labels.
