## Strategic idea

Hengshi Design is an **evidence-led innovation delivery partner** with the exact
promise **From complex ambition to accountable delivery**. The retained Signal
Ledger system provides mineral calm, controlled luminous energy, visible proof,
and structured progression. Framework Relay replaces the founder-rejected
Signal Ledger logo with four open, Quiet Framework-derived modules around a
non-diagonal stepped evidence relay and square checkpoint. It is a coherent
reconciliation: governed modularity frames accountable movement.

The identity never implies a delivered outcome, customer, office, certification,
sector history, award, or market superiority. Immersion demonstrates craft;
semantic content supports understanding and decisions.

## Identity principles

1. Evidence has stable text, shape, line, source, and date form.
2. Motion shows relationship, sequence, state, or orientation.
3. Mineral calm carries the surface; luminous precision marks few meaningful signals.
4. Full semantic text leads every entity, service, evidence, status, and action.
5. Precision remains warm, candid, and human-scale.
6. Distinctiveness comes from original structure and coherence, not novelty claims.
7. Recovery, reduced motion, monochrome, print, low power, and Quick Access are
   complete brand expressions.

## Logo rules

The current family comprises:

- `logo-framework-relay-primary.svg` — paper/cyan lockup on Ink;
- `logo-framework-relay-light.svg` — Ink/light-cyan lockup on Paper;
- `logo-framework-relay-mono.svg` — one-color Ink lockup;
- `logo-framework-relay-reverse.svg` — one-color White lockup;
- `mark-framework-relay.svg` — exact 80-unit standard mark;
- `mark-framework-relay-small.svg` — bounded 24-unit optical mark; and
- `favicon-framework-relay.svg` — bounded 16-unit optical favicon.

Use the full name at first contact, in metadata and structured data, for
legal/accessibility contexts, and whenever entity disambiguation matters. The
standard mark uses the exact `framework-relay-master-80-v1` geometry. Clearspace
is 20 units, twice the 10-unit module. Minimums are 168 CSS px/38 mm for the
horizontal lockup, 32 CSS px/7 mm for the standard mark, 24 CSS px for the
optical small mark, and 16 CSS px for the favicon. The 144 CSS px lockup is
rejected because its 11.66 CSS px name falls below the 12 CSS px floor.

Use flat Ink, Paper, White, or Black backgrounds. Do not stretch, rotate,
outline, bevel, extrude, glow, gradient-fill, crop, recolor individual modules,
close the brackets, diagonalize the relay, or turn the checkpoint into a
decorative particle. No shield, globe, leaf, pickaxe, tactical, sparkle,
circuit, customer, location, award, or certification variant is permitted.
Full construction rules are in [the logo specification](03-logo-system.md).

The iteration-2 Signal Ledger logo family is preserved only in
`archive/iteration-2-signal-ledger-logo/` with its hashes and rollback meaning.
It is superseded, not a current alternative or approved production asset.

## Color

Core tokens are Ink `#0B0F14`, Graphite `#161D26`, Slate `#2A3542`, Stone
`#D8D3C8`, Mist `#F3F1EA`, Paper `#FAF9F6`, and White `#FFFFFF`. Signals are Cyan
`#00D4FF`, Amber `#FFB000`, Lime `#B6F36B`, and Violet `#A98CFF` on dark
surfaces, with light-surface equivalents Cyan `#006D82`, Amber `#8A5600`, Lime
`#4F7D18`, and Violet `#6D52B5`.

Paper on Ink is 18.25:1; Mist on Graphite 15.01:1; Cyan on Ink 10.86:1; Amber on
Ink 10.49:1; Lime on Ink 14.66:1; Violet on Ink 7.21:1; Slate on Paper 11.83:1.
Signals occupy a small part of a composition and never encode meaning alone.

## Typography

No font software is packaged. Display and text use
`system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Arial, sans-serif`.
Evidence metadata uses `ui-monospace, "Cascadia Mono", "Segoe UI Mono", Consolas,
monospace`. Availability and metrics vary by platform. No paid or hosted font is
approved. Body text defaults to 16 CSS px or larger with 1.5–1.7 line height;
essential text never drops below 12 CSS px. Use sentence case and full formal names.

## Imagery, layout, and iconography

Prefer consented documentary process, human-scale work, material detail, and
clear system explanation. Every substantive media item requires origin, creator,
rights, evidence type, caption/alt decision, checksum, and approval state.
Agriculture and mining require reviewed context and avoid pastoral, rescue,
extractive, machinery-spectacle, or universalizing tropes. Defense and tactical
imagery are absent. AI-generated media is not included and can never represent a
real person, place, customer, system, or result.

Use a 12/6/4-column responsive grid, 4 px base unit, and 8–96 px spacing rhythm.
Asymmetry may suggest progression while DOM and reading order remain logical.
Icons use a 24-unit grid and 2-unit stroke; minimum display size is 24 CSS px.
Every ambiguous icon has visible text and an accessible name.

## Motion, sound, and 3D

Signal reveals run 180–320 ms, state transitions 120–200 ms, and context shifts
240–400 ms. Reduced motion shows the complete stable state without travel,
parallax, loop, particle, or camera move. Essential content never waits for a
transition. Audio is off until informed choice, has persistent mute, and is
never the only navigation, status, or evidence channel. Meaningful speech needs
captions and a transcript. No sound asset is included.

Suggested materials are ink basalt, warm limestone, brushed graphite, narrow
signal glass, and paper ceramic. They are conceptual treatments, not sourced
materials or a scene. No protected prototype geometry, texture, mesh, material,
or layout is reused. Lighting must preserve text contrast without bloom, fog,
reflection, transparency, or HDR dependence. Full formal titles, current
location, destination, exit, Quick Access, booking, quality, audio, and
accessibility remain visible and keyboard operable. Every WebGL panel maps to
equivalent canonical semantic content.

## Accessibility, SEO, social, and print

Target WCAG 2.2 AA; do not claim conformance before independent evidence. Keep
complete meaning at 200% zoom/320 CSS px reflow, keyboard and screen reader use,
forced colors, grayscale, reduced motion, non-WebGL, unavailable font, print,
and asset failure. Meaning never depends on color, material, motion, depth,
sound, or location alone.

Use the full name consistently for entity clarity. Visual headings never replace
complete initial semantic HTML. The optional `/world` expression remains
nonindexable and points to canonical routes. Organization logo structured data
is held until the mark, entity facts, rights, trademark, and release are
approved. Social compositions require a truthful page subject, full name,
licensed/versioned assets, safe-crop testing, and release consistency. Print
defaults to one color or high-contrast neutral pairings; physical process and
color proofs remain later gates.
