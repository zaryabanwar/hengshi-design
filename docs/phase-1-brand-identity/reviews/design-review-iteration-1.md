## Actionable findings

### BID-DR-I1-001 — MEDIUM — The declared master construction does not match the supplied SVG geometry

**Requirement references:** D-006; D-026; BR-006; `01-identity-principles.md`
principles 2 and 6; `03-logo-system.md:14-27`.

**Location/reproduction:** `03-logo-system.md:16-23` specifies a 64-unit square
with 8-unit rails at x=10 and x=46, an 8-unit joining field, a 4-unit signal path,
and a 6-unit evidence point. `assets/logo-signal-ledger-primary.svg:6-11` and the
light/mono variants instead use rails at x=8 and x=54 with width 10, a 10-unit
bridge, a 5-unit signal stroke, and a 9-unit-diameter point. The monogram uses a
third proportional construction.

**Impact:** A later designer, trademark drawing, Figma library, motion system,
favicon redraw, sign, engraving, or 3D wayfinding asset cannot reconstruct one
authoritative mark. Unrecorded proportion changes will produce visual drift and
rework across media.

**Acceptance condition:** Establish one authoritative geometric master and make
every supplied variant derive from it, or document each optical deviation with
explicit scale/context rules. The construction table, SVG coordinates, exported
variants, and machine-readable geometry must agree, with a deterministic check
that catches future drift.

### BID-DR-I1-002 — MEDIUM — The stated minimum sizes are not supported by the asset geometry

**Requirement references:** D-006; D-026; BR-006; UX-001; NFR-006;
`03-logo-system.md:69-88`; `04-color-and-typography.md:108-113`.

**Location/reproduction:** Scaling the 560-unit lockup to its declared 144 CSS px
minimum makes the 34-unit name approximately 8.74 px and the 11-unit descriptor
approximately 2.83 px. At the declared 38 mm print minimum they are approximately
2.31 mm and 0.75 mm respectively. Scaling the 32-unit favicon to 16 CSS px makes
its 3-unit cyan signal stroke 1.5 device px at DPR 1, below the package's own
2-device-pixel small-mark rule. No at-scale raster or physical proof is included.

**Impact:** The descriptor becomes unreadable, the entity name falls below the
package's meaningful-text guidance, and the diagonal can blur or weaken in the
smallest browser state. The published minima would cause inconsistent or
illegible production use.

**Acceptance condition:** Optically test and show the lockup at actual 144 px and
38 mm output, define the breakpoint at which the descriptor is removed, and
raise the minimum size if needed. Redraw the 16 px favicon on whole-pixel geometry
so every meaningful stroke meets the declared minimum, then provide 16/20/24/32
px DPR-1 evidence plus the separately required accessibility checks.

### BID-DR-I1-003 — MEDIUM — Production specimens use undocumented colors outside the semantic palette

**Requirement references:** D-006; D-026; BR-006; UX-001;
`04-color-and-typography.md:5-67`; `semantic-tokens.json:10-41`.

**Location/reproduction:** The light Signal Ledger asset uses `#007B91`
(`logo-signal-ledger-dark.svg:10-11`), Quiet Framework uses `#B06D00`
(`logo-quiet-framework.svg:10`), and the icon/data specimen uses `#006D82`,
`#007B91`, `#4F7D18`, `#6D52B5`, and `#8A5600`
(`icon-data-specimen.svg:8-18`). None appears in the documented palette or semantic
tokens. The validator proves that the eleven advertised values occur in the docs,
tokens, and board CSS; it does not prove that SVGs use only those values.

**Impact:** Light-surface, evidence-state, and data implementations have no named
token, contrast contract, or permitted-use rule. Teams may either copy hidden
values from specimens or use the advertised luminous colors and fail on light
surfaces, fragmenting the identity and accessibility evidence.

**Acceptance condition:** Define and name any required light-surface/accessible
derived signal tokens, publish their contrast pairings and allowed roles, and use
them consistently in Markdown, JSON, CSS, and SVG; or replace the undocumented
values with approved tokens that meet the required contrast. Extend validation to
reject undeclared non-monochrome asset colors.

### BID-DR-I1-004 — MEDIUM — Evidence-state encodings are not stable across guidance, tokens, HTML, and SVG

**Requirement references:** D-007; D-019; D-026; BR-008; PUB-006; PUB-007;
`01-identity-principles.md:9-15`; `04-color-and-typography.md:58-70`.

**Location/reproduction:** The canonical table specifies an **open diamond** and
dash-dot rail for a demo (`04-color-and-typography.md:63`), but
`icon-data-specimen.svg:10` fills the diamond while labeling it open; the HTML
board uses an open `◇` (`index.html:88`). The canonical table specifies a
**double rail** for conditional/review-needed (`04-color-and-typography.md:65`),
the HTML and SVG use a long dash (`index.html:89`, `icon-data-specimen.svg:11`),
and tokens permit the ambiguous value `double or long dash`
(`semantic-tokens.json:87`). The documented Error state has no corresponding
`semantic-tokens.json` evidence-state entry.

**Impact:** Proof type can change shape or line grammar between semantic, data,
immersive, and machine-generated surfaces—the exact ambiguity this identity says
it prevents. The missing Error token also makes an essential recovery state easy
to omit in downstream systems.

**Acceptance condition:** Select one exact marker and line pattern for every
state, including Error, and make the normative table, semantic tokens, HTML, SVG,
alt/description text, and later implementation contract identical. Add validation
that checks the complete state set and canonical marker/line values.

### BID-DR-I1-005 — MEDIUM — Founder comparison evidence does not demonstrate the claimed identity systems or required scale/media states

**Requirement references:** D-006; D-012; D-026; BR-006; UX-001 through UX-005;
`02-visual-directions.md`; `03-logo-system.md:122-130`;
`07-accessibility-seo-and-applications.md:22-34`.

**Location/reproduction:** B and C define distinct layout, typography, color, and
motion systems (`02-visual-directions.md:53-99`), but their only visual assets are
single lockups using the same generic wordmark format. The board references A/B/C
lockups, A primary, the data specimen, and OG specimen only
(`visual-boards/index.html:35-111`); it does not show the recommended light,
monochrome, reverse, monogram, favicon, 16/24/32/144 px, forced-colors, grayscale,
or print states. The manifest PNG is a 1440 × 1100 opening-viewport capture that
ends before the direction cards become visible.

**Impact:** Mark-level differences are visible, but the package does not evidence
that A/B/C are meaningfully different identity systems, nor that the recommended
system holds together in the specific scale/media states the founder is being
asked to approve. This turns part of the selection into an inference from prose.

**Acceptance condition:** Add one bounded, equal-format application specimen for
each A/B/C expression showing its claimed layout/type/color behavior, while
keeping B/C subordinate. Expand the recommended-system comparison with actual
light, mono/reverse, monogram, favicon, and at-scale outputs. Provide full-board
wide/medium/narrow captures and the required forced-colors, grayscale, and print
evidence, with checksums and no external dependencies. A static preview intended
for founder review must include the comparison itself, not only the masthead.

### BID-DR-I1-006 — MEDIUM — The deterministic validator cannot support the required revision/re-review loop

**Requirement references:** D-003; OPS-001; Constitution principles VII and VIII;
`README.md:73-83`; `handoff.md:40-46`.

**Location/reproduction:** `validation/validate-brand-identity.ps1:297-299`
requires both `reviews/` and `accessibility/` to contain zero files. Creating this
required independent report—or the separate accessibility report—therefore makes
the producer validator fail even when every producer artifact is unchanged. The
workflow simultaneously requires reviewer reports to be preserved and prohibits
the producer from editing them.

**Impact:** Producer iteration 2 cannot achieve the required deterministic pass
without deleting or moving independent evidence, which would violate the review
contract and break auditability.

**Acceptance condition:** Revise the validator so it permits the exact expected
independent reports from prior passes, verifies that they remain outside producer
write scope and unchanged by recorded hashes, and still rejects producer-prewritten
or unexpected reviewer artifacts. The revised validator must pass on the preserved
iteration-1 reports before independent re-review begins.

## Subjective founder choices — not defects

The following are legitimate direction choices and must not be treated as
evidence-backed findings:

1. Whether Signal Ledger's cyan-on-ink technology character is appropriately
   distinctive or should become warmer/more restrained after the objective token
   and evidence issues are corrected.
2. Whether the deliberately generic system wordmark is sufficient for the desired
   premium character or a future custom/paid type exploration is worth its separate
   cost, rights, privacy/performance, and accessibility gate.
3. Whether Signal Ledger, Quiet Framework, or Resonant Field is the preferred
   identity expression after equal comparison evidence exists.
4. Whether the mineral/architectural tone feels composed versus austere,
   masculine-coded, or extractive after representative human-scale agriculture
   and mining imagery is available for cultural review.
5. Whether the exact selected mark should proceed to independent trademark/legal
   search. This report cannot authorize or predict clearance.
