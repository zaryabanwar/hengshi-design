## Coherence, regression, and production-feasibility assessment

### Identity coherence and CR-001 fidelity

Framework Relay remains one Quiet Framework-derived family rather than a pasted
Direction B mark: four open modules establish the framework, while the central
joining rule is replaced by the Signal Ledger-compatible non-diagonal stepped
relay and square checkpoint. Primary, light, monochrome, reverse, standard,
24-unit optical, and 16-unit favicon forms remain frozen against the nine exact
geometry anchors. The broader Signal Ledger palette, typography, asymmetric
layout, evidence rails, imagery/data boundaries, motion, sound, 3D implications,
semantic HTML, Quick Access, and non-WebGL principles remain unchanged.

The board consistently presents Signal Ledger as the **retained system**, Quiet
Framework as **source exploration**, Framework Relay as the sole **current
candidate**, the prior H-derived logo as **superseded**, and Resonant Field as
**closed historical**. There is no reintroduced A/B/C decision or identity
redesign.

### Evidence, action, and responsive meaning

The six evidence states retain exact text/marker/line grammar. The five
illustrative series retain direct labels and unique marker/pattern mappings that
do not duplicate a complete evidence-state combination. The two actions remain
`Open Quick Access example` and `Review optional immersive principles` in every
relevant browser record. Wide, medium, narrow, 320, text-spacing, asset-failure,
forced-colors, grayscale, and print modes retain content priority and meaning.

### Brand and UX alignment

The result remains aligned to D-026: mineral calm dominates, luminous signals
are sparse and meaningful, evidence state is explicit, full-name-first entity
clarity remains, and the promise/category are exact. No unsupported customer,
outcome, office, expert, award, certification, superiority, sector-history, or
production claim appears. Recovery modes remain authored brand expressions,
not reduced-content fallbacks.

### Feasibility and asset quality

The SVG family is cleanly reproducible from the machine-readable geometry, and
the supported digital size transitions are explicit. The 144 px lockup remains
correctly rejected. The 38 mm lockup and 7 mm mark remain conditional on later
physical-process proof. The manifest and clearance language accurately limit
all assets to local internal review; no public or production use is implied.
