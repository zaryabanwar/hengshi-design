# Phase 1 Brand Strategy Slice

**Project:** Hengshi Design

**Snapshot:** 2026-07-19 (Asia/Karachi)

**Status:** Producer iteration 2, ready for independent review after bounded
revision; founder approval has not been inferred

## Scope and authority

This slice translates the founder-accepted Phase 1 foundation (D-025) into a
usable brand strategy. It recommends a strategic direction, provides two genuine
alternatives, and defines the positioning, promise, messaging, voice, proof, and
claim boundaries required by later identity, UX, content, and design teams.

Authority is applied in this order: D-001 through D-025; the constitution and
project governance; the accepted artifacts in `docs/phase-1-foundation/`; then
dated reference research and protected prototype evidence. The public name
remains **Hengshi Design**. The existing application and exterior GLB are
protected, non-authoritative prototype evidence.

This is strategy only. It does not create or approve a logo, palette, typography,
design token, UI, motion system, 3D scene, Figma/Stitch output, public claim,
publication, external write, or implementation. D-006's architectural
mineral-neutral direction is retained only as a later identity implication.

## Fixed strategic inputs

- Position as an evidence-led, premium, platform-oriented innovation delivery
  company for global English-speaking mid-market and enterprise buyers.
- Address leaders responsible for technology, data, product, transformation, and
  innovation. This is an audience target, not proof of a global footprint or
  enterprise client history.
- Lead with agriculture, follow with mining, and keep defense absent from public
  messaging until MA-011 is fully satisfied.
- Sell strategy-to-delivery custom programs through discovery and proposal, with
  no fixed public pricing.
- Use a qualified 30-minute discovery booking as the primary conversion; AI,
  WebGL, proof review, and live help are optional. Email verification and booking
  confirmation are process states, not qualities of the future conversation.
- Keep verified Work and Hengshi-owned Demos separate. Unverified prototype seeds
  are not proof.
- Make complete semantic HTML, Quick Access, accessibility, and recovery
  first-class. Immersion is an optional enhancement.

## Acceptance boundary

The producer may make this slice ready for independent review but cannot approve
it. Final category framing, positioning, promise, direction, naming changes,
legal or regulated claims, and strategic departure require founder approval.
Brand identity remains blocked until the strategy direction is explicitly
approved after a clean independent review. Direction A is fully elaborated in
this slice. A preference for Direction B or C requires bounded reconciliation of
the A-specific strategy, messaging, and implementation artifacts, deterministic
revalidation, and a new independent review before strategy approval or identity
work can begin.

## Compact founder decision presentation

**Recommended:** **A — Evidence in Motion**. It balances enterprise decision
clarity and proof discipline with memorable immersive distinction, while keeping
the semantic journey primary.

- 👍 / **A** — After a clean independent review, approve the fully elaborated
  **Evidence in Motion** strategy for later identity, UX, and content definition.
- 🧭 / **B** — Prefer **Assurance Architecture**, a more conservative
  enterprise-trust lead; this is a directional preference, not strategy
  approval.
- ✨ / **C** — Prefer **Immersive Catalyst**, a more expressive craft-led lead.
  This is a directional preference, not strategy approval.

An A approval is valid only after independent review reports no unresolved
CRITICAL, HIGH, or MEDIUM finding. A B or C preference triggers bounded
reconciliation of the A-specific strategy, messaging, and implementation
artifacts, deterministic revalidation, and a new independent review; it does not
approve strategy or unlock identity. No choice approves identity assets, exact
public copy, claims, publication, implementation, or launch. Brand identity
remains blocked until the selected, reconciled strategy passes clean independent
review and receives explicit founder approval.
