## Accessibility implications for later identity

Accessibility is a brand behavior and release condition, not a separate mode or
compliance badge.

### Identity requirements

- The full name and formal service titles must remain legible without logo
  interpretation, animation, texture, or spatial placement.
- Later color and typography choices must target WCAG 2.2 AA across text,
  controls, focus, status, data visualization, and disabled/error states.
- D-006's architectural mineral-neutral direction must retain distinguishable
  states without relying on subtle low-contrast neutrals alone.
- Meaning must not depend only on color, material, depth, glow, sound, or motion.
- A brand mark must have text and small-size alternatives, but no mark is selected
  in this strategy.

### Voice and content requirements

- Use plain global English in essential paths; define technical abbreviations and
  terms on first use.
- Keep sentences and headings meaningful when read out of visual sequence.
- Avoid direction-only instructions such as “enter the blue room” or “see left.”
- Label AI as AI, Demos as Demos, unavailable spaces as unavailable, and status
  changes explicitly.
- Error and recovery copy states what happened, what was preserved, and what the
  visitor can do next without blame.

### Experience requirements

- Quick Access is the canonical responsive, search, keyboard, assistive-
  technology, low-power, and recovery journey—not a reduced-content fallback.
- Every meaningful immersive action maps to semantic navigation and a visible
  canonical link.
- Motion must communicate relationship, state, or progression; reduced-motion
  users receive an equivalent authored state without nonessential travel.
- Audio begins only after an informed choice; meaningful media has captions or a
  transcript.
- Focus, status, verification, consent, booking, and failure behavior must remain
  understandable without canvas or animation.

## SEO and entity implications for later identity

- Use the full “Hengshi Design” name consistently to reduce confusion with
  unrelated Hengshi entities.
- The public category descriptor should remain stable across homepage identity,
  About, metadata, approved profiles, and Organization/WebSite context once
  approved.
- Visual headlines cannot replace meaningful initial HTML, one visible H1,
  landmarks, crawlable links, breadcrumbs, and truthful metadata.
- Formal service titles and Agriculture/Mining names are keyword/entity
  authority; decorative signage cannot change them.
- Optional `/world` presentation links to canonical semantic routes and stays
  nonindexable.
- Social imagery, logos, organization facts, people, profiles, and media schema
  remain held until rights and evidence are verified.

## Cultural and sector risks

| Risk | Treatment |
|---|---|
| English idioms, war metaphors, or culture-specific wordplay obscure meaning for a global audience. | Use plain international English in essential messages; test comprehension across regions. |
| “Design” or immersive craft is read as surface styling rather than accountable delivery. | Pair expression with exact service scope, evidence type, and decision value. |
| Agriculture is portrayed as uniformly rural, low-tech, pastoral, or in need of rescue. | Use reviewed, context-specific language; avoid stock stereotypes and universal claims. |
| Mining is romanticized as extraction, scale, machinery, or risk spectacle. | Center reviewed operational, human, environmental, reliability, data, and trust context without implying expertise or moral judgment. |
| “Global” becomes a colonial, borderless, or one-size-fits-all delivery claim. | Define it only as intended English-speaking audience and acknowledge local context where evidence supports it. |
| “Agentic” implies uncontrolled autonomy or replacement of human responsibility. | State governance, evidence, human, data, and refusal boundaries wherever the service is explained. |
| Architectural/mineral language becomes austere, masculine-coded, inaccessible, or literally extractive near mining content. | Treat the direction as a material/structural identity boundary, not sector imagery; test warmth, contrast, cultural associations, and nonvisual equivalents. |
| Defense language leaks into the public brand through “mission,” “command,” “arsenal,” “war room,” or similar metaphors. | Avoid militarized metaphors in public messaging and keep all actual defense content quarantined. |

## Implementability for downstream teams

The implementation guidance below elaborates Direction A. It may guide identity,
UX, content, motion/3D, product, AI, and booking definition only after a clean
independent review and explicit founder approval of Direction A. A Direction B or
C preference requires bounded reconciliation of the A-specific strategy,
messaging, and implementation guidance, deterministic revalidation, and a new
independent review before any chosen direction can unlock identity.

### Identity team

Translate “Evidence in Motion” into a system that combines structure, depth, and
controlled energy. Preserve the architectural mineral-neutral boundary, but do
not select exact palette, typography, logo, iconography, materials, motion tokens,
or sound in this artifact. Every concept must show semantic, high-contrast,
small-size, reduced-motion, and monochrome behavior.

### UX and information architecture team

Use the messaging hierarchy to design progressive specificity from home to
service/sector/proof/trust and booking. Offer Quick Access and optional immersive
entry at the first meaningful choice. Keep exact formal names visible and test
the category, promise, evidence labels, CTA expectations, and empty states.

### Content and SEO team

Assign one dominant intent and accountable reviewer to every canonical page. Use
the route-family map, exact taxonomy, proof labels, conditional grammar, and claim
review questions. Never create thin service-sector intersections to fill the
route plan.

### Motion and 3D teams

Use motion and space to explain connection, progression, evidence type, and
location. Do not make spectacle a gate to content. Define reduced-motion,
keyboard, low-power, asset-failure, non-WebGL, skip, audio, and exit behavior
before approval. The protected exterior GLB is reference-only unless MA-004 is
satisfied.

### Product, AI, and booking teams

Use the same promise and evidence status in semantic, AI, human, and booking
paths. AI states when it cannot verify and never binds scope, price, timeline, or
legal terms. Booking describes the qualification and verification process
truthfully and never claims success before reconciliation.

## Strategy conformance questions

A downstream concept conforms only if all answers are yes:

1. Can a buyer identify Hengshi Design, the category, relevance, proof status,
   and next step without animation, WebGL, audio, or specialist jargon?
2. Does it express clarity and imagination without an unsupported comparative or
   future-as-fact claim?
3. Does it preserve the exact service/sector architecture and Work/Demos
   separation?
4. Are semantic, keyboard, reduced-motion, low-power, and asset-failure routes
   equivalent in content and conversion?
5. Are claims, people, proof, entity facts, media, and trust statements tied to
   the correct evidence gate?
6. Does the concept remain culturally intelligible and avoid agriculture,
   mining, global, AI, and defense stereotypes?
7. Can it be implemented, tested, and rolled back without relying on the
   protected prototype as production authority?

If any answer is no, revise the concept before requesting approval.
