**Status:** Draft foundation slice for independent review; not Phase 1 acceptance

## 1. Decision and Evidence Boundary

This strategy translates D-005 through D-010, D-017, D-019, and D-020 into a
founder-reviewable search contract. It does not authorize implementation,
publication, DNS changes, crawler submissions, external accounts, or unsupported
claims. The machine-readable route authority for this slice is
[`06-canonical-route-inventory.json`](06-canonical-route-inventory.json); claim
eligibility comes from
[`05-evidence-and-claims-ledger.json`](05-evidence-and-claims-ledger.json).

Current market, reference-site, and official search guidance is recorded in
[`02-market-competitor-seo-evidence.md`](02-market-competitor-seo-evidence.md).
Keyword phrases below are hypotheses, not measured demand. This package contains
no invented search volume, ranking, traffic, market-share, or conversion forecast.

### Outcomes

1. Establish one unambiguous Hengshi Design entity around verified facts.
2. Make every approved service and sector discoverable as complete semantic HTML.
3. Help a qualified buyer understand fit, evidence, trust, and the next step
   without requiring WebGL, AI, or an account.
4. Convert relevant organic discovery into a verified 30-minute qualified
   discovery booking while minimizing personal data.
5. Keep drafts, fictional seeds, rejected claims, sensitive data, and unapproved
   AI knowledge out of search and public answer systems.

### Non-goals

- Ranking promises, traffic guarantees, bulk page generation, doorway pages, or
  keyword-volume assertions.
- Fabricated locations, reviews, awards, partners, experts, cases, or outcomes.
- Paid links, link networks, bulk directory submissions, or reciprocal-link
  schemes.
- Bot-specific HTML that differs materially from the visitor experience.
- Making the optional 3D campus a prerequisite for discovery or conversion.
- Publishing defense content or routes before MA-011 is satisfied.

## 4. Canonical Route Architecture

The route inventory contains 33 unique planned or reviewable routes/patterns and
nine explicit exclusion classes. Planning, content approval, release activation,
indexability policy, and active-sitemap membership are separate states. In this
Phase 1 snapshot no route is content-approved, release-active, or in an active
sitemap. Its key rules are:

- Canonical host: `https://hengshidesign.com`.
- Lowercase, hyphenated, stable paths; no trailing slash except `/`.
- English only at launch. No `hreflang` until real translated URLs exist.
- Complete semantic HTML is present before client JavaScript for every indexable
  route.
- `/world`, admin, preview, draft, rejected, chat, session, internal-search,
  tracking variants, filter/sort variants, staging, and defense surfaces are
  absent from active public sitemaps; their status, canonical, and indexing rules
  remain distinct where their behavior differs.
- No fabricated geographic routes.
- Each 3D room links to its canonical semantic service page; canonical service
  pages may progressively link into a relevant 3D state without depending on it.

### 4.1 Route families

| Family | Routes | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Entity | `/`, `/about`, `/contact` | Identity, evidence, operating approach, verified contacts |
| Service | `/services`, five wing hubs, ten service pages | Complete offer discovery and fit evaluation |
| Industry | Proposed `/industries` hub, agriculture, mining | Evidence-backed sector/service intersections |
| Proof | `/work` and verified detail pattern | Authorized client work only |

The `/industries` collection hub is the only route in the inventory marked as a
derived proposal for explicit founder review. It creates a stable parent and
breadcrumb target for the two approved industry pages; omitting it would require a
redirect/internal-linking decision before implementation.

### 4.2 Internal linking contract

1. Once their content is approved and their exact routes are active, the homepage
   links visibly to Services, Agriculture, Mining, approved Work or
   Demos, Insights, Trust, About, Contact, and Book as those collections become
   publishable.
2. `/services` links to every wing; each wing links to each contained formal
   service; every service links back through crawlable breadcrumbs.
3. Service pages link only to relevant approved industry contexts, verified work,
   labeled demos, insights, experts, trust evidence, and booking.
4. Industry pages link to relevant services and evidence; they must not repeat
   generic service copy or imply sector delivery history.
5. Work and demo pages link to the actual services, industry context, evidence,
   expert, and limitations represented visibly.
6. Insights link to the responsible verified expert, relevant service/industry,
   primary sources, related trust topic, and a proportionate next step.
7. The optional 3D campus links to canonical HTML pages through ordinary anchors.
8. Every indexable page must be reachable through crawlable navigation without
   site search, JavaScript-only handlers, canvas raycasting, or a generated form.
9. Anchor text describes the destination; avoid repetitive exact-match stuffing.

## 5. Rendering, Indexing, and Publication Contract

### 5.1 Semantic-first output

Every indexable route must return, in its initial HTML response:

- a unique, descriptive `<title>`;
- a unique meta description appropriate to the visible page;
- one clear primary heading and a logical heading structure;
- a self-referencing canonical URL on the approved host;
- a truthful robots directive;
- visible body content that satisfies the route intent;
- ordinary anchor links and breadcrumbs;
- visible author/reviewer/date/evidence where the content type requires them;
- Open Graph/social metadata and a licensed social image when available;
- JSON-LD that matches visible content and the approved schema policy.

Static prerendering or equivalent deterministic HTML generation is preferred.
Client hydration may enhance interaction but must not supply the only meaningful
content, metadata, links, consent, help, or booking path. Do not serve materially
different content to crawlers and visitors.

### 5.2 Atomic release

The lifecycle remains:

`Draft -> specialist evidence/SEO review -> founder approval -> render/index -> public`

One immutable publication checksum must tie together responsive HTML, metadata,
structured data, redirects, sitemap entries, AI knowledge, and 3D panels. Draft,
rejected, or superseded content cannot enter public HTML, sitemaps, search
notification, social metadata, or AI retrieval. A failed render or indexing step
keeps the previous approved release active.

### 5.3 Canonical and duplicate handling

- Every indexable public document has exactly one canonical clean URL. A
  nonindexable utility or variant follows its explicit machine-readable exclusion
  rule and is not assigned an unrelated canonical merely to satisfy this rule.
- HTTP, `www`, case variants, and unwanted trailing slashes redirect in one hop to
  the canonical URL.
- Only allowlisted tracking parameters may canonicalize to the clean document;
  they never create active-sitemap entries, and first-party internal links remain
  clean.
- Internal search is a `noindex,follow` utility with canonical mode `omit` in the
  current proposal. Filter/sort URL parameters are not approved for launch and
  return `404` until a later inventory revision defines an allowlist and behavior.
  These classes must not be collapsed into a `noindex or canonical` choice.
- Paginated collections, if later needed, require a separate visible-navigation
  and canonical decision; do not silently canonicalize distinct useful pages to
  page one.
- Cross-domain canonicals require an explicit syndication decision and evidence.

### 5.4 Status and retirement behavior

| Condition | Required response |
|---|---|
| Permanent one-to-one replacement | Server-side `301` or `308` to the closest equivalent approved route |
| Temporarily unavailable | Truthful `503` with bounded recovery behavior; do not redirect everything to home |
| Missing/unpublished unknown route | Real `404` with useful navigation; never soft-404 with `200` |
| Intentionally and permanently removed with no replacement | `410` after editorial/legal approval |
| Draft/rejected/preview | Access-controlled and `noindex`; absent from sitemaps and AI/search notifications |
| Retired approved article with a true successor | One-hop redirect plus updated internal links and redirect ledger |

Redirects are versioned publication data. Chains, loops, broad irrelevant
redirects, and client-only redirects are release blockers.

## 7. Metadata and Media Rules

1. Titles describe the page and include “Hengshi Design” where useful for entity
   clarity; avoid boilerplate that obscures the distinctive page topic.
2. Descriptions summarize the visible value and next step without claims that are
   absent from the page. They are snippets, not ranking guarantees.
3. H1, title, canonical, breadcrumb, Open Graph title/description, social image,
   and JSON-LD entity must agree on the page subject.
4. Social images must be licensed, versioned, readable at target crops, and use
   truthful text. No client logos or outcomes without approval.
5. Images use descriptive filenames, dimensions, responsive variants, and useful
   alternative text; decorative imagery uses empty alt text.
6. Video pages expose meaningful text, captions/transcripts, controls, thumbnail,
   and fallback. A video or canvas-only page is not an indexable content strategy.
7. Media sitemap fields are generated only for approved visible media.

## 8. Sitemap, Robots, and Crawler Policy

### 8.1 XML and media sitemaps

- Generate active sitemaps from the approved active publication manifest, not the
  planning inventory or mutable authoring database.
- Include only canonical `200` indexable URLs from the active release.
- Do not include redirects, `noindex`, `404`, `410`, preview, session, filter,
  staging, or unauthorized content.
- Use accurate modification dates derived from substantive approved publication
  changes, not every build.
- Add image/video sitemap data only for eligible visible licensed assets.
- Publish and reference the sitemap index in `robots.txt`; submit externally only
  after MA-009 and launch approval.

### 8.2 Robots and indexing separation

`robots.txt` manages crawling; it is not access control and does not guarantee
de-indexing. Sensitive/private surfaces require authentication/authorization.
Pages that search engines must remove need a crawlable `noindex` response or an
appropriate status code, not a robots block that prevents the directive from
being read.

The production crawler registry must be versioned and reviewed quarterly. This
dated evidence/control matrix is a proposal, not a production directive:

| Class and current example | Documented purpose | Does `robots.txt` apply? | Are uses separable? | Proposed default, evidence date, and gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Automatic conventional search — `Googlebot` | Google Search crawling/indexing | Yes, subject to Google's documented crawler behavior | Search crawling is a distinct documented crawler class | Permit approved public releases under abuse controls; verify from [Google common crawlers](https://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/crawlers-fetchers/google-common-crawlers), checked 2026-07-19; founder/security release gate remains |
| Automatic answer/search discovery — `OAI-SearchBot` | OpenAI search discovery and result links | Yes | Separately documented from `GPTBot` | Consider permitting approved public releases; verify from [OpenAI crawler documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots), checked 2026-07-19; founder/legal/privacy/security gate remains |
| Training crawler — `GPTBot` | Content that may be used to improve/train models | Yes | Separately controllable from `OAI-SearchBot` in current documentation | Block unless separately approved; same OpenAI source/date; founder/legal/privacy/security gate remains |
| User-triggered fetcher — `ChatGPT-User` | A fetch initiated by a user action | Robots rules may not apply according to current provider documentation | It is not an automatic search or training crawler control | Do not represent it as `OAI-SearchBot` or rely on robots for private content; public/auth boundaries control access; same OpenAI source/date and gates remain |
| Mixed-purpose product token — `Google-Extended` | The documented Gemini model-use purposes controlled by one product token | Yes as a robots product token; it is not a separate request user agent | No separate switch for the coupled documented uses under the current token | Treat the uses as one policy decision; do not claim “allow grounding, block training” separately; Google source/date above; founder/legal/privacy/security gate remains |
| Unknown or abusive automation | Unverified or abusive requests | Unknown until identified | Unknown | Rate-limit or block through an evidence-based security decision; never infer provider identity |
| Private/draft/admin/session content | Not public content | Irrelevant to the security boundary | Not applicable | Never public; authentication, authorization, and release isolation apply regardless of crawler identity |

Exact request identities and tokens are temporal configuration, not timeless
product copy. Resolve them from official provider documentation immediately before
release, record whether robots applies and whether purposes are separable, test
representative responses, and avoid accidentally blocking ordinary search.
IndexNow activation and webmaster submissions remain gated by MA-009.

### 8.3 Deterministic query-variant rules

The machine route inventory controls these classes independently:

| Class | Current status/index rule | Canonical rule | Internal-link and sitemap rule | Parameter rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Internal search | Approved utility may return `200` with `noindex,follow`; unknown search paths return `404` | Omit canonical on the nonindexable result document; result items link to clean canonical pages | No crawlable search-state navigation; never in active sitemap | No URL query parameter is approved in this foundation |
| Recognized tracking parameters | Inherit the clean document's truthful status and are not a separate indexable route | Strip only the documented allowlist and emit the clean document canonical | First-party internal links stay clean; variants never enter active sitemap | `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_term`, `utm_content`, `gclid`, and `msclkid`; unlisted parameters require a new decision |
| Filter/sort variants | No URL parameter is approved now; unapproved variants return `404` | Do not canonicalize an unapproved subset/reordering to the collection | No crawlable parameter URLs; never in active sitemap | Empty until a reviewed route-inventory revision defines valid values and `200`/`noindex` behavior |

The validator rejects ambiguous `*_or_*` indexability, an undocumented
`noindex` plus clean-document canonical combination, and any query-class entry
without explicit status, canonical, link, sitemap, and parameter rules.

## 9. Editorial Standards and Launch Content

### 9.1 People-first evidence rule

AI may help organize research and drafts, but every substantive publication needs:

1. a documented buyer question and route intent;
2. a named qualified subject reviewer or an explicit blocked state;
3. primary evidence and claim-level citations where appropriate;
4. original Hengshi analysis, framework, demonstration, data, or decision value;
5. limitations, update date, owner, and review cadence;
6. evidence/SEO specialist review independent of the author;
7. founder approval before render/index/public.

Do not publish paraphrased consensus pages, search-result summaries, thin service
variants, city pages, programmatic intersections, anonymous “expert” copy, or
articles created merely to capture keywords. AI-generated text receives no special
privilege or automatic penalty in this contract; usefulness, originality,
accuracy, evidence, ownership, and policy compliance control publication.

### 9.2 Minimum launch content

- Homepage, Services hub, five wing hubs, and ten complete service pages.
- Agriculture and mining industry pages; the `/industries` parent is a founder
  route decision.
- Honest Work and Demos collection states, with detail pages only for approved
  entries.
- About, verified experts, Trust Center, Contact, and Booking where required facts
  exist.
- Six cornerstone expert-reviewed insights.
- No fixed pricing.
- No defense content.

The north-star search outcome is **qualified organic discovery**, not raw traffic.
The conversion definition and formulas live in
[`04-conversion-measurement-model.md`](04-conversion-measurement-model.md).

| Direct help/AI/human-assisted organic bookings | Understand optional assistance | Aggregate transition events; no conversation content in analytics | No baseline |

Analytics remain first-party, cookieless, aggregate-only, and data-minimized. Do

analytics. Retention/deletion policy applies to lead data, while publication and
aggregate operational records follow their separately approved class. Numerical
conversion targets, attribution windows, lead-scoring weights, and sales SLAs
require founder approval after a trustworthy baseline exists.
