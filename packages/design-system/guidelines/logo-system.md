# Logo system

Framework Relay is the current logo candidate: four open governed brackets define a reviewable frame, a non-diagonal stepped evidence relay shows accountable progression, and a square checkpoint identifies the current evidence point without claiming completion. Every lockup, mark and favicon is **EXPLORATORY, UNREGISTERED, TRADEMARK NOT CLEARED**: founder selection and independent trademark review remain required before adoption, structured-data use, publication, filing or production mastering. The mark is not a shield, certification, platform product, building, client outcome or legal conclusion.

## Construction

`geometry-spec.json` in the source is the single machine-readable authority (`framework-relay-master-80-v1`). The standard mark is an 80 by 80 viewBox:

- four open bracket paths `M30 10 H10 V30`, `M50 10 H70 V30`, `M70 50 V70 H50`, `M30 70 H10 V50`, stroke 6, square caps, mitre joins;
- the stepped relay `M16 52 H32 V40 H48 V28 H64`, stroke 5, square caps, mitre joins;
- the square checkpoint at x 61, y 25, 6 by 6;
- no diagonal, enclosing border, shield, badge, letter, gradient, glow or implied tick.

The horizontal lockup viewBox is 0 0 420 128; the master is translated by 24, 24; the wordmark "Hengshi Design" starts at x 124 with a 34-unit size, weight 700, letter-spacing -0.8, baseline y 69. Category and promise are separate semantic text, never inside the logo.

Only two optical deviations exist, both shipped as files: `mark-framework-relay-small.svg` (24-unit grid, 2-unit strokes, three-step relay, checkpoint kept) for 24 to 31 CSS px, and `favicon-framework-relay.svg` (16-unit grid, whole-pixel strokes, checkpoint removed because the relay endpoint carries it) for 16 to 23 CSS px. Any other geometry change needs a new authority, review and founder decision.

## Full name first

Use "Hengshi Design" in first contact, page and entity headings, metadata, accessible names, structured data, legal contexts, credits, social identity and anywhere the mark could be ambiguous. The mark alone never defines the entity, a service, a state, a control or an evidence status. Below 168 CSS px use the full semantic name in text rather than a forced lockup.

## Clearspace and minimum sizes

One unit **x** is a 10-unit framework return in the master. Keep 2x (20 master units) clear on every side of the mark and outside the lockup; the 20-unit internal gap between mark and name is never reduced. No rule, crop, image, evidence badge, status marker, motion path or other bracket enters the clearspace, and the logo brackets are never repeated as a surrounding UI or card frame.

A 144 CSS px lockup is a rejected comparison (name 11.66 CSS px, relay 1.71 device px).

## Colour, monochrome and reverse

On ink: paper brackets with the on-dark cyan relay and checkpoint (`hd-color-signal-cyan-on-dark`). On paper: ink brackets with the on-light cyan (`hd-color-signal-cyan-on-light`). Colour never changes geometry or evidence meaning. Monochrome is one solid ink for brackets, relay, checkpoint and name; reverse is one white ink on black. The stepped path and open brackets must stay distinguishable in office print, low-bandwidth documents, emboss, laser etch and single-ink fabrication.

## Backgrounds

Prefer flat ink, graphite, paper, mist, white or black fields with declared contrast. On photography use an opaque approved field; never rescue contrast with shadow or glow. Never place the logo over mineral texture, detailed imagery, data plots or 3D reflections. Do not recolour brackets by service wing, sector, evidence state or status. The square checkpoint is a logo element, not the Proposed evidence-state square.

## Prohibited

No stretching, rotating, skewing, outlining, bevelling, extruding, gradient, glow, continuous animation, crop, badge or shield enclosure, or corner rounding. No return to the superseded diagonal Signal Ledger logo and no use of the Quiet Framework source exploration as the current logo. No checkmark, seal, approval badge, platform glyph, agriculture leaf, mining mountain or pickaxe, tactical symbol, globe, AI sparkle, circuit trace, client, location, award or partner variant. No registration, filing, merchandise, public structured-data logo, shared library or production use before the applicable human gates.
