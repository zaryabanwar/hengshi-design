## Review Rule

The coordinating project manager updates probability/impact only when new evidence
exists. A reviewer may close a risk only when its stated exit evidence is durable.
Residual risk acceptance requires an explicit founder decision in
`docs/decisions-log.md` and `DECISIONS.md`.
