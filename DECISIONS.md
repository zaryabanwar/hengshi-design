## D-045 — Delivery-stream model accepted, with four founder resolutions

**Date:** 2026-09-06
**Status:** accepted
**Supersedes:** the `stream_profile` model introduced at D-042 and amended at D-043
**Authority for:** producer implementation of the delivery-stream evidence model
and the stream accessibility obligations

Under D-044 the producer did not author this model. Two authors independent of the
producer, and of each other, delivered it as
`docs/phase-1-ui-reference-design/DELIVERY_STREAM_EVIDENCE_MODEL.md` and
`docs/phase-1-ui-reference-design/accessibility/DELIVERY_STREAM_ACCESSIBILITY_OBLIGATIONS.md`.
The founder accepted both on 2026-09-06 and resolved the four contested points
below. The producer now implements, and only implements.

**1. Gate G-1 — the two accepted statements are separated, not overruled.**
Capability and connection conditions select which **stream** is in force, per
D-039. Entry into the **World** remains explicit, per `UX_ARCHITECTURE.md:173`.
These were never in conflict once the stream and the canvas are treated as
different things. The first frame of `ROUTE-HOME` is therefore a non-World shell
carrying the delivery stream control, with the capability-selected stream already
in force and advised, and the World entered only on explicit action. Neither the
style guide nor the UX architecture is overruled; §8.2.2 is amended to stop
describing `S-LOW` as an unsignalled default for the *canvas*.

**2. Invariance — `DS-STREAM-INVARIANT` is deleted, not derived.** The design
author's reasoning governs: a zero-member guarded category is a place for an
unexamined record to hide later. The *test* survives as a validator assertion; the
*category* does not. The accessibility author's `OBL-INV-01` derivation conditions
are retained as the reasoning that justifies the deletion and are recorded in the
validator source beside the assertion, so a future record that would qualify is
recognisable without the category existing to absorb it. This removes the cell
that the producer filled derivably in D-042 and again in D-043.

**3. Stream control — the concurring role and activation model is normative.**
Four native radio inputs in a `fieldset`/`legend`, plus a separate always-present
submit; two-step explicit confirmation. Arrow, Tab, and pointer movement change
only which radio is **checked** and must not apply a stream, reload, or alter the
tree beyond checked state. The group name reports the **in-force** stream while
the checked radio is the **pending** value, and the two must be programmatically
distinguishable. A native `select` applying on `change`, and any
listbox/combobox/menu/radio group applying on selection, blur, arrow movement, or
timeout, are prohibited. Native radios are chosen over a listbox with an Apply
button because role, checked state, group membership, arrow semantics, and
forced-colors rendering come from the platform, removing four independent 4.1.2
failure opportunities. Advance advisement is **persistent visible text** inside
the group, preceding the submit in reading and visual order, programmatically
associated with both group and button, never tooltip/hover/focus-only; an
accessible description alone is insufficient.

**4. Frame budget — full merged evidence set, calls batched and approved
individually.** The union of both authors' required frames is the evidence
obligation. **No call projection is offered or recorded**, because CB-09 forbids
one before B01 measures and the metered unit is the call, not the frame. Actual
Figma calls are batched at the coarsest useful granularity per CB-13 and each
requires explicit founder approval per CB-06. The countervailing saving is
recorded: B01's current `DS-S-*` obligations are World-shaped strings attached to
non-World templates and are therefore unsatisfiable, so a producer would either
omit frames and fail review or fabricate them and fail `EC-18`/`SC-01`. This slice
is cheaper than releasing B01 as it stands.

**Scope.** Design and production packages are amended in the **same slice**, per
D-044, because per-stream evidence obligations in the design package are
meaningless while the production package cannot name a per-stream frame.

**What this decision does not do.** It does not accept the implementation. The
producer implements to an accepted specification and the reviewers then verify;
Constitution principle VII is unchanged and **R-035 stays open**. **B01 stays
held.** Gate **G-5** — `time_limit_branch` is a total function of `state_profile`
via `Get-ExpectedTimeBranchForState`, the identical derived-column defect — is
recorded and **not** fixed in this slice; it is outside both authors' mandate and
the producer is no longer permitted to author model unilaterally.

and its freeze hash intact** — one edit made during the slice was reverted — the gap
is logged as risk **R-039**, and the amendment is commissioned later as its own
bounded specialist contract. The exposure is real and stated: a stream control
cannot be drawn in B01 without tokens that do not exist, so R-039 must resolve
