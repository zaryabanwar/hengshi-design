Booking status: pending, confirmed and refresh failures

Reviewed definition · visual translation proposed The booking service owns the result. This explanation shows no appointment, live calendar, working controls or selected response-time promise.

First booking: pending

Choosing a slot or submitting a request does not confirm an appointment. While submission is unresolved, show pending and no confirmed occurrence. A timeout cannot turn it into confirmed or failed.

Confirmed: reconciled evidence

Show a confirmed appointment only when local and approved calendar/meeting evidence agree. Display its 30-minute time in the visitor’s local timezone with an unambiguous label. A successful status read alone is not booking confirmation.

Change requested: still pending

A reschedule or cancellation remains pending until reconciled. Keep the last confirmed occurrence clearly labelled as last reconciled; do not show the requested replacement as confirmed or promise that the external calendar is unchanged.

Cancellation: reconciled

Only completed reconciliation can show cancelled with no active occurrence. This does not mean booking records were deleted, consent was withdrawn or a conversation ended. Those have separate rules.

  1. Read your current bookingCheck the current permitted process. A lineage reference identifies a record; it cannot grant access or recover a lost session.
  2. Refresh without changing itThe proposal allows a small bounded burst of reads while a booking or change is pending. Refresh never submits again, calls calendar operations or extends access. Errors stop automatic refresh.
  3. Leave or lose access safelyHidden/offline views and leaving the process stop the burst. Returning does not silently resume it. Lost access stops private display; late replies cannot restore an old process or overwrite newer status.
  4. Recover honestlyIf access remains usable but a read fails, any permitted prior snapshot is last known. Offer manual Refresh or Contact. Do not automatically create another booking; an uncertain previous intent needs its own reconciliation.

“I could not refresh the status” is different from “your booking failed.” A pending result, network error or capacity refusal cannot invent a booking outcome.

The read, response shape and corrected transport have separate bounded independent reviews. The numerical work/rate/polling limits are unmeasured proposals. Reschedule/cancel authority and cutoff rules, production screens, actual HTTP/store/calendar/client enforcement and integrated/founder acceptance remain held. This presentation does not select the separate unresolved visitor-chat lifetime or change staff-session limits.
