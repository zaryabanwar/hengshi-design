## Incident-response rehearsal — v1

Status: DOING. Phase 1 scenario definition, 2026-10-09. The
[incident response contract](SECURITY_PRIVACY_CONTRACT.md#incident-assessment-escalation-and-recovery--v1)
controls the duties. These are future isolated synthetic rehearsals and actual
boundary checks after approved implementation, not prose tests or drills already
executed. Independent review and integrated acceptance remain required.

Prepare synthetic incident facts, a controlled clock and approved simulated
notification/operating interfaces. Never send an exercise to a real authority,
visitor, mailbox or provider. Do not introduce real credentials or personal data.
Before an operational rehearsal, freeze the applicable role/authority matrix,
processing scope, notification routes, copy policy and recovery profile. Record
which boundaries were exercised versus simulated; a tabletop discussion cannot
prove the application, provider delivery, security or production recovery.

| Scenario | Required observed decision/outcome |
|---|---|
| Report is incomplete, duplicated or reaches general Contact | Preserve first receipt and evidence lineage; restricted escalation and prompt triage; no report in public content, AI context or general enquiry views; duplicates/reassignment do not reset supported awareness. |
| Suspected intrusion is initially unconfirmed; later facts establish compromise | Record suspicion and established awareness separately with reasons/uncertainty. Investigate promptly; no invented investigation grace period. Never wait for complete forensics, staff approval or proof of exfiltration to recognize an evidenced breach. |
| Data are altered, lost or unavailable with no confirmed disclosure | Assess integrity/availability and harm to people as well as confidentiality. Successful restore alone cannot prove no breach or no notification duty. |
| Initially assessed unlikely risk; new facts increase risk | Preserve earlier assessment and justification; reassess recipients/duties promptly against the original supported awareness. A new case/owner or amended impact cannot silently restart the clock. |
| A processor reports a breach involving Hengshi-controlled records | Separate the processor's prompt controller report from the controller's risk assessment/authority decision; capture actual awareness evidence and incomplete details without waiting for a full provider report. |
| Required authority notification has incomplete counts or misses 72 elapsed hours | Initial known information and explicitly pending facts, phased follow-up without undue further delay, actual competent recipients and reasons for delay; no weekend pause or automatic extension. |
| High-risk individual communication or claimed Article 34 exception | Separate authority and individual decisions; validate applicable protective facts, plain useful communication and required equally effective alternative where relevant. No blanket encryption/no-contact exception. |
| Multi-country affected people, Pakistan entity and EU hosting/representative | Apply the qualified establishment/territorial route; no invented European office or regulator chosen merely from hosting. Where the non-EEA rule applies, all required affected-state recipients are covered. |
| Notification channel returns timeout, late response or confirmed rejection | Keep draft/attempt/unknown/definitive outcomes distinct; reconcile approved content and recipients; no guessed delivery, false completion or unqualified blind retry. |
| Wrong-role staff inspect evidence or try emergency/publication override | Deny at the actual protected read/action boundary, including list/search/projection. Mailbox, administrator or combined content roles do not grant incident authority. Preserve a safe outcome without sensitive payloads. |
| Restore precedes containment or reintroduces expired/withdrawn material | Admission remains held until current security, suppression, publication fencing, provider reconciliation and recovery-profile evidence permit service. Preserve I/R/V/F and awareness separately; a successful restore command is insufficient. |
| Closure, later reopening and evidence preservation | Record assessment/disposition and current notification/recovery outcomes without falsifying earlier clocks or readings. Only specifically necessary, authorized evidence is retained under its reviewed finite purpose/copy policy; no unlimited archive or ordinary-use retention reset. |

For an executed case, evidence must show facts available at each decision, actual
authorized actor/action, clock source/uncertainty, risk and routing rationale,
content/recipient identity, definitive or unresolved channel outcome, and affected
service admission/recovery result. Redact evidence by approved purpose; neither a
large check count nor a fabricated receipt is acceptable. An unknown or missing
required boundary remains unproven rather than counted as passing.

Legal notification timeliness is assessed separately from NFR-005 RPO/RTO. The
accepted recovery targets and D-068 provider/cost deferral are unchanged. Owners,
actual contacts, applicable legal routing and operating mechanisms remain held
before live use; none is assigned or activated by this scenario definition.
