Prove the four-mode interactions before the full design build

The next proposed Figma trial checks that choosing a mode, applying it, closing a panel and returning to a screen behave consistently. It covers all four choices on navigation, search and recovery.

Approval scope: a new, labelled area in the existing private Figma file. The existing trial and original designs remain intact. This page explains a proposed trial; it is not the working website or a new visual-design acceptance.

What I will inspect

  1. Every current/pending combination, including selecting the current choice again.
  2. Apply, repeated changes and preservation of the underlying context.
  3. Menu/search Close, reopening, Escape and inactive search background.
  4. Recovery context, with no invented panel closing or live staff claims.
  5. Scroll continuity from a visible nonzero position.
  6. Preserve versus reset, repeated after entering another flow.
  7. Actual keyboard behavior, with unavailable native semantics reported honestly.
  8. New-node ownership, source safeguards and editable exports.
What remains afterward: failure/refusal and timing scenarios, complete responsive and accessibility references, restore checks, the remaining design batches, independent result review and acceptance of the complete Phase 1 package.
