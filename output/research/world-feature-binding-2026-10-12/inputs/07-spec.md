## Problem, Audience, and Evidence

Visitors must be able to understand Hengshi, inspect approved evidence, request
help and book discovery through either the semantic website or an explicitly
entered World. Europe is the first target market without an implied local office.
The founder selected mouse/touch free movement with nearby streaming and an
original nature-integrated campus. The protected application and Atlanta exterior
do not provide the accepted production experience. The current original campus
study provides three inspectable views, but lacks connected collision geometry,
spatial streaming, movement, sound and an accepted close appearance match.

## User Journeys and Failure Journeys

### Primary journey

1. Read the semantic homepage and choose services, evidence, Contact or Book.
   The approved stream control remains available on that page.
2. Explicitly choose Enter World; resolve an eligible release and apply the
   supported stream under accepted capability/preference rules. Quick Access
   continues to the equivalent semantic destination without creating a canvas.
3. Arrive at the dominant reception with usable Stop, Exit and stable travel
   alternatives. Select a destination or steer/walk with the accepted mouse/touch
   controls once their final reference has been approved.
4. Enter an eligible department over ready connected ground. Read sourced boards,
   use clearly identified AI guidance, or request a human with current availability
   and consented context sharing. Direct Book is independently available.
5. Reverse, retarget, return to reception or leave for the corresponding semantic
   destination. Movement cannot change the applied graphics stream by itself.

### Accessibility and non-WebGL equivalent

1. Quick Access provides equivalent content, destinations and complete help/booking
   processes without WebGL, sound or movement skill. Indexable pages are complete
   semantic HTML before JavaScript.
2. Keyboard-accessible actions expose destinations, boards, help, Book, Stop and
   Exit. Touch/drag alternatives and reduced-motion stable destination changes
   are required; a gaming-key scheme is not the accessibility substitute.
3. Informational panels have semantic equivalents and deliberate focus handling.
   Camera motion, gesture, sound or an avatar cannot be the sole information cue.

### Failure and recovery

1. Late or failed loading holds the last safe position and offers visible loading,
   Stop, retry and Quick Access. It does not imply the service itself is closed.
2. Closed/unready destinations refuse entry and explain the actual state with
   usable eligible routes. Never place a visitor over missing ground or water.
3. Ordinary release refresh preserves an eligible visit pin until safe context
   replacement; withdrawal/unknown eligibility stops affected travel, reads and
   sound. Discard late responses from abandoned or ineligible contexts.
4. Preserve authoritative private handoff/booking lineage and permitted process
   context. A scene refresh, retry or sound failure cannot resubmit a booking,
   fabricate confirmation or erase an active private process.

## AI and Human-Handoff Requirements

Use the accepted reception concierge and contextual approved-knowledge guidance;
identify AI, preserve sources and refuse unsupported commitments. Do not create a
second agent policy or treat model context as eligibility. Handoff remains the
existing availability/consent/assignment process, with optional voice/video under
its own opt-in and alternatives. No availability/response promise is inferred
from a board, desk or scenic character. Direct Book works without chat/handoff.

## Acceptance Scenarios

1. **Given** a semantic first visit, **when** the visitor applies a stream without
   Enter World, **then** the homepage remains usable and no World entry occurs.
2. **Given** an eligible ready area, **when** the visitor moves, reverses or selects
   another destination, **then** only safe ground is crossed and Stop remains usable.
3. **Given** active travel, **when** a panel opens, input cancels or visibility is
   lost, **then** travel stops and decorative audio pauses as applicable.
4. **Given** a delayed/missing neighboring area, **when** travel reaches its boundary,
   **then** the last safe position is retained with loading/retry/Quick Access.
5. **Given** a held room or invalid position, **when** entry/recovery is requested,
   **then** a truthful eligible route is offered without unsafe placement.
6. **Given** different graphics streams, **when** the same service is requested,
   **then** essential content, signs and help/Book processes remain equivalent.
7. **Given** a newer eligible release and active private booking/handoff, **when**
   the scene refreshes, **then** the target destination is safely resolved and the
   private lineage/outcome is preserved without duplicate submission.
8. **Given** withdrawn/unverifiable public context or late old responses, **when**
   they arrive, **then** affected reads/travel/audio stop and cannot revive that context.
9. **Given** sound never enabled, muted, failed or ineligible, **when** the journey
   continues, **then** decorative ambience stays silent with complete essential
   content/actions; separately consented human media keeps its own controls.
10. **Given** enabled ambience and permitted resumption, **when** zones/visibility
    change, **then** master preferences remain and no startup cue/autoplay bypass occurs.
11. **Given** unavailable staff or declined context sharing, **when** human help
    is considered, **then** no waiting person is implied and browsing/Book remains usable.
12. **Given** the same original source model, **when** its three views and routes
    are inspected, **then** geometry agrees, the reception/wing/valley hierarchy is
    present, and final appearance is explicitly accepted rather than inferred.
13. **Given** a first wing without its required foundation/reception/concierge/
    handoff/direct-Book prerequisite, or a later wing whose required predecessors
    have never opened in the same or a prior active approved release, **when** a
    visitor selects its threshold, **then** public traversal is refused with the
    truthful held/closed state and usable eligible semantic recovery. A visible
    study volume, private candidate preview or approved-looking panel is not an
    opening record. Once prerequisites and current wing eligibility are satisfied,
    entry still requires ready connected ground. The canonical wayfinding map
    decides which historical/current opening records satisfy the sequence.
14. **Given** a visitor has reversed toward recently crossed ground while an
    obsolete area request is pending, **when** residency work evicts an unrelated
    area, **then** current/required adjacent ground and active interaction context
    are retained, stale request results cannot replace them, and shared resources
    still owned by a resident area are not destroyed. If the accepted residency
    budget cannot support safe travel, hold the safe position and offer recovery
    rather than cross missing ground or silently change the graphics stream.
15. **Given** an active World with movement, enabled ambience and pending area
    work, **when** the visitor exits, **then** World travel/playback stop, obsolete
    work cannot later reactivate the scene, and resources no longer owned by a
    live context are released under the approved cleanup envelope. Allowed private
    handoff/booking lineage and master preferences retain their own policy; Exit
    cannot fabricate a booking outcome or end a human process without its required
    explicit action. Equivalent semantic navigation/focus remains usable. This
    requires ownership/cleanup evidence, not an invented immediate-zero RAM target.
16. **Given** exploration, reversal, room selection and diagnostics, **when**
    network, storage, telemetry and retained records are inspected, **then** no
    new durable exact-position/trajectory history or device fingerprint is
    generated, transmitted or stored by this feature. Only the permitted session
    state and approved aggregate measurement remain. Private guidance/handoff
    receives only its separately authorized context, not an implicit movement
    trace. A tracking configuration that violates this boundary is rejected as
    an unacceptable feature configuration; it does not gain authority from a
    decorative scene or an ordinary asset download.
