## Native mechanisms and execution guards

Basic component variants, explicit click/key triggers and Open/Swap/Close overlay
are candidate mechanisms. Pending changes must not mutate the in-force state.
Apply may reach only a target with the correct new active label and context.
Inspect retained scroll/open-panel behavior rather than infer it from identical
screens. Where the approved destination has no corresponding surviving subtree,
depict the approved fallback and focus transition explicitly.

Native action/target capabilities, evidence identities and state-retention behavior
remain qualification gates before wiring. Do not depend on paid prototype-variable
actions, conditionals, multiple-action logic, Make or plugins. MCP is capped and the
agent composer refused daily credits; this plan authorizes no prompt or retry.

The timer is a visibly synthetic design fixture. More time and the notice are
available from the beginning; warning allows at least 20 seconds and at least ten
extension opportunities. Pending/stale/refused responses grant no invented new
deadline. End/expiry cannot lead back to active private access. Do not fabricate a
ticket, credential, restored transcript or absolute visitor conversation limit.
