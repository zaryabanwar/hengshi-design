## Authority and the visual target

The [project charter](../../PROJECT.md) defines five wings and ten services.
The [wayfinding map](../phase-1-ux-architecture/wayfinding-release-map.csv) owns
their exact names, canonical links and release sequence. D-065 accepts the

D-067 selects nature-integrated organic architecture. These later decisions
supersede conflicting earlier navigation/material implications only in that scope.

The target is a thriving natural valley with advanced technology quietly integrated
into organic architecture. A visibly dominant central reception connects five
distinct sculpted wings through gardens, paths and terraces. A river and layered
terrain extend into the distance; mountains must not form an artificial boundary
wall. This expresses a design vision, not a real office location or measured
environmental claim. Timber/plant/earthen appearance is a virtual material language;
it is not a claim that every real technology can contain only organic materials.

The [nature-campus concept board](concepts/nature-campus-direction-2026-10-04.png)
supplies the founder-selected visual direction. It is not an exact
floor plan: differences among its views must be resolved into one coherent model.

proportions, entrances, roof openings, landscape levels and room relationships.

## Movement and access

Selected behavior: free steering/walking with mouse on desktop and touch on mobile,
alongside destination navigation. WASD is not selected or required. The World is
not a game; content and conversion cannot depend on movement skill.

PROPOSED desktop mechanics: drag an empty scene area to look; select a valid floor
point to move along a collision-safe path, with a persistent Stop action and the
ability to retarget or reverse while moving. A separate explicit movement control
supports continuous travel in the chosen direction without overloading content
clicks. No mouse capture is required to read a panel or leave the World.

PROPOSED touch mechanics: drag the scene to look; a visible movement pad supports
continuous travel, while single-tap destinations and Move/Stop alternatives avoid
requiring simultaneous gestures or dragging. Final gesture mapping remains a
reviewable UI choice; do not silently treat this proposal as approved screen design.

Collision-safe traversable ground, entrance clearance and accessible connections
are separate authored geometry from the visual mesh. The visitor cannot fall into
water, pass through walls or enter closed/unready rooms. Routes offer orientation,
return to reception and safe recovery from invalid positions. No jumps, timed
challenges, collectibles or hidden content gates are introduced.

Keyboard-accessible DOM actions provide equivalent destinations, panels, help,
booking, Stop and Exit; every camera gesture need not be duplicated as a gaming
key. Reduced motion suppresses nonessential camera travel and provides stable
destination changes. Quick Access contains equivalent core content and processes.
Opening a panel stops movement; focus and pointer cancellation must not leave
travel latched. Tab/application visibility loss stops travel and pauses ambience.

## Spatial streaming versus graphics streams

The accepted four graphics streams remain governed by D-039/D-040/D-045 and their
current controls. Do not introduce automatic in-session tier switching. WebGL
capability ceilings, preference rules and accepted user override precedence remain.
No universal browser score or untested memory/FPS threshold is claimed here.

PROPOSED scene structure: separately exported reception, wing interiors, nearby
exterior landscape tiles, shared assets and distant scenery. The visitor's current
area, immediate connected neighbors and a recently traversed buffer stay resident.
Prefetch follows position, direction and connected entrances; direction reversal
reprioritizes requests without immediately unloading the area behind the visitor.

Never let the visitor cross into unloaded collision/ground geometry. If loading is
late, hold the last safe position with visible loading status, Stop, retry and
Quick Access; do not expose an empty world or falsely imply a closed service.
Use distance hysteresis and a measured memory ceiling before eviction; protect
current/adjacent ground and active interaction state. Cancel obsolete requests
and bound concurrency. Network/cache/asset failure must preserve semantic journeys.
Offline inspection is not promised when required assets are absent.

The [campus build specification's loading and residency contract](CAMPUS_ASSET_BUILD_SPEC.md#loading-and-residency-contract)
is the proposed controlling engineering refinement for preparation versus safe
admission, reversal priority, shared-resource leases, late/cancelled work, refresh
overlap and renderer-context recovery. Keep those details there rather than
duplicating a scheduler state machine in this journey definition. Its later
review does not replace the exact inputs of the earlier World/feature reviews.
Measured profiles, controls, geometry and actual runtime evidence remain required.

Every chunk and asset belongs to the same immutable published release. Hash/version
checks reject mismatched assets. A public scene cannot fetch drafts or activate
unreviewed content by loading a new tile. Foliage detail, shadows and distant scenery
may vary by graphics stream; routes, service content and essential controls cannot.

### Release changes during a visit

Follow the proposed [publication refresh/eligibility contract](../phase-1-architecture/contracts/PUBLICATION_CONTRACT.md#visitor-release-refresh-and-eligibility)
and the accepted mid-session branch in [FLOWS.md](../phase-1-ux-architecture/FLOWS.md).
An ordinary newer release allows the current safe interaction to finish only
while its pin remains eligible, with a refresh announcement. Chunk, panel,
collision, answer/source and enabled sound context must not silently switch
independently. Cached assets and a valid checksum do not grant eligibility.

PROPOSED safe scene refresh: stop travel and prepare the complete target context
before replacing the scene/panel context. Resolve the visitor's logical destination
against the target release, including its opening sequence and safe ground. Do not
reuse raw coordinates when geometry has changed. If the destination is closed,
retired or lacks ready collision, explain that state and offer an eligible
canonical page, reception or Quick Access rather than placing the visitor in
water, a wall or an unloaded room. Exact refresh/control presentation still needs
the approved UI reference; this proposal selects no forced camera animation.

Cancel obsolete loads and reject late completions by their release/request
identity. Shared resources can be reused only if their immutable bytes/dependencies
are verified for the target; ownership/disposal must not free geometry needed by
the current safe interaction. Retain master sound preferences, while releasing
ineligible audio and scene resources. A declined or failed sound asset cannot
prevent the silent target journey.

Urgent withdrawal or an expired/unverifiable pin stops affected travel/reads/audio;
if selective suppression is unproven, leave the release's public presentation
and retain stable semantic recovery controls. No offline eligibility promise.
Keep already-entered process state only under the approved privacy policy, and
reconcile any existing handoff/booking by its authoritative lineage. A scene
refresh cannot submit again, cancel an appointment or turn pending into confirmed.

## Sound

The founder selects nature ambience outside, quieter interiors and contextual
department layers as visitors progress. Audio remains off until informed user
activation. PROPOSED zones follow the visitor's actual spatial context:

| Zone | Intended sound | Required behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Arrival and landscape | Breeze, birds and water where the landscape supports them | Optional nature ambience; distant scenery or a loaded tile cannot independently trigger more loops. |
| Reception | Quieter ambience reflecting the sheltered atrium | Transition from outdoors without implying an active call, receptionist or conversation. |
| Department interior | Quiet room ambience with a restrained contextual layer | Context can vary with the department, but sound cannot identify its service, availability or evidence state alone. |
| Guidance, handoff and booking | No decorative imitation of human communication | Any real speech/media requires the accepted captions/transcript and non-audio equivalents; no ringing or simulated staff voices. |

PROPOSED zone transitions blend a bounded set of loops, preserving master
mute/volume and suppressing abrupt restart on turns or tile reloads. Muting stops
audible playback; tab/application visibility loss pauses ambience. Declining
audio must avoid eagerly downloading optional sound assets. Playback eligibility
requires user intent and the active release; a scene chunk becoming resident is
not consent. Restore enabled ambience only when the context and browser permission
allow it, without replaying a startup cue. No autoplay bypass is proposed.

If an audio asset fails or loses release eligibility, retain a silent equivalent
journey; do not substitute an unrelated track or reset the visitor's preference.
Sound assets use the existing publication artifact/eligibility authority, not an
independent content store. Exact formats, playback implementation, loop counts,
crossfade durations and decoded-memory budgets remain measurement/review inputs.
Pure decorative ambience has no hidden informational role; meaningful speech and
cues follow the accepted caption/transcript and non-audio-equivalent requirements.
No simulated incoming call or invented human activity. Missing/declined audio
does not remove content. Asset rights and any paid licensing need their gates.

### Audio lifecycle and ownership proposal

PROPOSED 2026-10-05: refine the selected optional ambience and F3D-12/13 here.
This is the controlling sound-lifecycle definition; the campus loading contract
continues to own release generations, reservations and shared-resource leases.
No new audio package, codec, sound asset, production implementation, telemetry,
recording or paid licence is selected. Exact numeric profiles and supported-device
proof remain acceptance inputs, not assumed defaults.

#### Visitor intent and browser state

Keep three independent facts: the visitor's ambience intent/master preferences,
the currently eligible World/zone generation, and the browser audio engine's state.
Browser permission or an already-running context is never evidence of opt-in.
Restored volume/mute preferences do not authorize sound in a new document. A
fresh document starts silent and requires informed activation; same-document
recovery retains intent but must recheck every applicable playback gate.

The playback gate requires explicit intent, unmuted nonzero master gain, visible
active World, ready eligible zone/assets, a valid current attempt and a usable
browser context. A room download, pointer-look gesture, destination click, AI
answer, staff availability or successful booking cannot enable ambience. Enabling
ambience does not consent to conversation retention, microphone capture, recording
or human context sharing. Keep separately consented speech/human media and their
controls outside the ambience bus; ambience failure/Exit cannot alter a booking
or handoff lineage or indiscriminately close another media owner's resources.

| Event / condition | Required ambience disposition |
|---|---|
| Never enabled, declined, or disabled | Silent; no optional audio transfer/decode/loop scheduling. Remain able to browse, move where ready, ask for help and book. |
| Explicit enable or retry | Request a usable audio context from the deliberate accessible control, then prepare only currently eligible bounded assets. A pending/rejected resume is not success and cannot trigger repeated automatic retries. |
| Muted or master volume zero | Suppress audible output promptly; invalidate pending play authorizations and stop new optional fetch/decode/scheduling. Retain preferences and only already-owned bounded cache entries. Late work cannot bypass mute. |
| Visible enabled eligible zone with usable context | Admit only matching prepared resources; announce the control's enabled state, not an unverifiable claim that device speakers are audible. OS volume/output remains outside that claim. |
| Hidden document, application visibility loss, page departure, or renderer loss | Stop new audio work and pause/silence the ambience bus. Cancel stale admissions and preserve preferences; a background timer cannot resume sound. |
| Visible return or valid same-document scene restoration | Revalidate World/release/zone, preference and resources. Resume only while intent remains valid and the browser allows it; otherwise show silent waiting/retry, with an explicit gesture if required. No startup cue. |
| Browser suspension/interruption or rejected playback | Stay silent/waiting; observe supported context state and recover without retry loops, new permission or unrelated replacement tracks. Do not require a browser-specific interruption enum. |
| Ordinary eligible release refresh | Retain preferences, stop old admissions and bind the new zone only with the complete target context. Old and new sound cannot independently switch releases or exceed overlap budgets. |
| Urgent withdrawal, unknown eligibility, or Exit to Quick Access | Silence affected sound and fence all pending starts immediately; release visit-owned resources. An acoustic tail/crossfade cannot justify continued withdrawn presentation. |
| Asset, policy, integrity, unsupported-format or budget failure | Preserve the silent equivalent journey and truthful retry state. Do not retry indefinitely, weaken browser policy, change graphics tier or substitute an unapproved sound. |

These are behavioral conditions, not an approved API enum or new preference-store
schema. Persistent control design follows the accepted brand/UX controls and needs
the existing production-screen gate. No storage key, new cross-tab synchronization,
server visitor profile or preference-retention duration is introduced here.

#### Context, sources and cleanup ownership

Prefer native Web Audio for the bounded ambience graph; evaluate a Three adapter
only if it brings measured spatial benefit within the complete stable graph.
One coordinator owns the ambience bus, current context lease and master gain.
Zones/React effects receive leases and cannot create an independent context per
building, close shared context on unmount, or register duplicate listeners/loops
through remount or development effect re-execution. This is an engineering
recommendation, not verified React/Three compatibility.

Native buffer source nodes are one-shot: a stopped source is retired and later
playback uses a new node referencing a still-valid owned buffer. Preserve only
bounded local loop-phase information where applicable; advancing context time,
paused offset and completed sources must not be confused. Disconnect retired
sources and remove their event handlers/references. An `ended` callback cleans its
own attempt only and cannot dispose a replacement source.

Muting/hidden-state pause suppresses this bus even if another audio owner keeps a
shared context running. Suspend a context only through its coordinator when no
other owner requires it; context suspension is not decoded-buffer cleanup. When
closing is appropriate, wait for closure before reusing capacity, release node,
listener and buffer references, and clear any closed context retained by the
adapter. Three's documented global context cannot be assumed to be per-World or
safe to close from a component. Do not create unbounded replacement contexts if
an earlier close/resume is still pending.

Transfer cancellation does not prove an already-started decode was cancelled.
Every pending operation carries visit/release/audio-intent/zone/context/attempt
identity; any change invalidates its admission right. Keep non-cancellable work
charged to the loading contract's draining/reservation accounting until it settles,
then discard stale results or release their owners. A successful fetch/decode or
resume promise alone can never start a loop without a fresh gate check.

#### Zone composition and resource bounds

Author sound zones against the same eligible reception/department destinations
and usable spatial boundaries. A camera turn, distant scenery, chunk reload or
prefetch cannot select a new acoustic room. Use the actual admitted visitor
context with reviewed boundary hysteresis; entering a held wing cannot activate
its sound. Clicked destinations and reduced-motion changes choose the same zone
as their equivalent admitted destination, without requiring a travelling animation.

Ordinary transitions may blend old/new eligible zone layers through owned gains.
Reserve the complete overlap, bound active sources, and replace obsolete automation
instead of accumulating queued ramps during repeated door crossings/reversals.
Master mute and all safety gates take precedence over a fade. Retain eligible
shared nature loops where appropriate rather than restarting them on every tile.
Actual fade shape/timing, layer/voice caps and musical loudness need authored
listening and named-device measurements; no universal constant-power formula or
physically simulated occlusion is claimed.

Sound metadata must bind exact eligible release/asset bytes, provenance, approved
zone use, codec/container/MIME, complete duration/channel/sample information and
reviewed loop bounds. Visitor/AI/content strings cannot choose arbitrary media
or decoder URLs. Admission uses the browser-resource policy and existing asset
registry; this is not a second public release manifest. Optional sound is excluded
from eager initial-page/World preloads and service-worker precaches unless a later
explicitly approved intent-aware contract permits it.

Account separately for encoded transfer, simultaneous encoded/decode inputs,
decoded sample buffers, node/source/automation owners and shared-cache references.
Float sample payload accounting uses channels × frames × four bytes; it is not a
claim about total browser/system memory. Decoding may resample to context rate;
compressed size alone cannot prove decoded memory or peak work. Unknown bounds
prevent admission under the reviewed finite profile. Complete-file buffer decoding
is not incremental world/audio streaming; long media would need a separately
reviewed media-element delivery path, rights and alternatives rather than an
unbounded decoded loop. No such additional media path is selected here.

Audio has lower admission priority than supporting ground, movement safety and
essential semantic/help processes. If its preparation cannot fit the measured
profile, remain silent and release audio-only ownership; never evict supporting
collision, strand the visitor or change the selected graphics stream to play sound.
No audio history, precise location trail, device fingerprint, microphone input,
provider audio transfer or new diagnostic-retention authority follows from this
proposal. Existing minimization and approved media alternatives remain binding.

#### Boundary evidence required after implementation approval

Exercise the real controls, network/decode boundary, audio graph and browser state;
a mock `running` value, schema fixture or documentation pass cannot prove playback
policy, silence, resource release or phone behavior. Include:

- Never-enable/decline before and during World entry: no optional audio requests or
  decoder/source creation, with full semantic/World help and booking available.
- Enable pending, then mute/volume zero/Exit/withdrawal: delayed resume, fetch,
  decode and `ended` events cannot start sound, reopen a zone or free its successor.
- Repeated enable/disable/remount and context teardown: no duplicate graph/listeners,
  stale global closed context, shared-owner interruption or unbounded outstanding
  decode/close work; buffers and peak reservations follow their actual owners.
- Rapid boundary reversals and release refresh: coherent zone selection, bounded
  overlapping gains/sources, no ramp buildup, no independent mixed-release audio
  and no crossing into a held/unready destination to complete an acoustic fade.
- Visibility loss, screen lock, supported interruption, renderer loss and restore:
  silent hidden state, preserved preferences, truthful waiting and permitted
  recovery without autoplay bypass, a startup cue or repeated resume polling.
- CSP/media denial, unsupported/truncated/corrupt/over-budget assets and network
  failure: silence plus usable retry/Exit/Quick Access; no arbitrary fallback track.
- Accessible control focus/keyboard state, audible level/transitions on actual
  output devices and required speech/media alternatives; emulated mobile layout
  cannot establish phone audio, memory, interruption or hardware behavior.

#### Primary research and review limits

Checked 2026-10-05 through Context7 `/websites/threejs` and official primary sources.
The [Web Audio 1.0 Recommendation](https://www.w3.org/TR/webaudio-1.0/)
supports native graph/source/gain mechanisms; publication of a standard alone
proves neither the exact supported browser matrix nor this product's behavior.
[Chrome's autoplay guidance](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/autoplay/)
explains browser activation constraints, not product opt-in or a cross-browser
permission guarantee. The proposed explicit enablement remains stricter than any
browser heuristic. Do not copy an example's eager load/play callback into the app.

[Page visibility](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Page_Visibility_API)
is distinct from focus/blur. [Context state](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/BaseAudioContext/state)
may reflect browser-specific interruptions; `running` alone does not prove audible
output or product authorization. [Buffer decoding](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/BaseAudioContext/decodeAudioData)
requires complete file data and may resample it. [Context closure](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/AudioContext/close)
releases system context resources without automatically freeing every retained
object reference. These observations motivate, rather than certify, the ownership
and recovery proposal above.

[Three Audio](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/Audio.html) exposes source controls;
[Three AudioContext](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/AudioContext.html) documents its
global native context. Current online docs are not exact selected-package or
source-to-binary proof. The experimental AudioSession API and draft-only Web Audio
features are not production requirements. No audio dependency, codec/asset, exact
wrapper configuration or physical-device profile is selected, and no audio runtime
test, sound generation, microphone use, permission/account change or application
edit was performed for this definition.

## Mouse/touch traversal and camera profile proposal — 2026-10-06

Status: DOING. This refines the proposed mechanics under [Movement and access](#movement-and-access)
and the accepted D-064 scope; it does not approve production controls or modify
D-071's layout/model. Exact numerical values below are unmeasured engineering
starting targets, not browser, accessibility, building-code or device-fitness
standards. Apply the same essential movement/clearance rules in every 3D stream;
Quick Access remains equivalent without WebGL. This is a client-side traversal
profile, not private-content authority or a second scene/publication manifest.

### Proposed visitor controls

Desktop default: primary-button drag on empty scene space looks around. An explicit
DOM Move action starts continuous travel in the selected forward/back/left/right
direction; a persistent Stop action ends it. Movement direction is camera-yaw
relative on the ground plane, never derived from camera pitch. While moving,
dragging the empty scene can steer by changing yaw; looking upward does not fly.
Single-click a valid ground point to choose collision-safe directed travel; clicking
a board/department/control invokes its own action instead of setting a floor target.
A floor request cancels continuous mode, and choosing continuous movement cancels
the prior route. Only one current travel intent exists. Right-click/context menu,
pointer lock, hover, wheel and a drag-release click are not alternate start actions.

Touch default: an optional visible thumb movement pad supports continuous planar
movement while engaged, and a scene drag controls look. Release/cancel of that pad
stops its travel. A one-pointer alternative uses the same explicit Move/Stop,
direction buttons, Look left/right/up/down, Reset look and destination controls;
the visitor need not drag or use two fingers simultaneously to reach equivalent
positions, orientations, content or processes. The pad cannot be the only way
to move, and keyboard controls alone are not the alternative for touch users.
Exact pad shape, pointer-down/drag cancellation semantics and comfort require the
approved B07 World-control reference and input/accessibility inspection before implementation;
this proposal does not claim its hold gesture already satisfies WCAG 2.5.2.

Move/direction/destination activation occurs on an uncancelled pointer-up or native
button activation, never a hover/down-only commitment. Stop is an abort control
and may stop earlier. Scene drag and pad gestures are explicitly owned by the
engaged pointer; cancel, lost capture or device interruption clears that intent.
Do not convert a drag into a synthetic floor/board click. Use temporary element
pointer capture only during the engaged gesture, releasing it on end/cancel;
no browser pointer lock or capture trap is required to read, use Stop or Exit.
Initial gesture separation target is eight CSS pixels for mouse and ten for touch;
these thresholds need physical-device/usability validation and cannot substitute
for native button, focus or assistive-technology activation.

Proposed camera sensitivity is 0.2 degrees per CSS pixel horizontally/vertically,
with Look buttons stepping yaw by 15 degrees or pitch by 10 degrees and Reset
look returning pitch to horizontal without moving the body. CSS pixels, not device
pixels, keep the gesture independent of rendering scale. Relative displacement
is consumed only from the current owned gesture, not from a stale pointer event.
Pitch is clamped and yaw wraps; there is no inertia after release. Drag sensitivity
and step sizes are comfort targets, not proven acceptable across devices.

Browser pinch zoom stays available; do not use user-scalable=no or disable zoom on
the whole document. Evaluate narrowly scoped touch-action for scene/pad gestures
without suppressing scrolling, pinch zoom, text selection or ordinary controls in
DOM panels. Browser takeover produces cancellation and stops travel. Do not assume
one CSS value has identical supported behavior on every required browser/device.
The inspection viewer's orbit and centre-target zoom stay inspection tools;
production walking uses the body/eye camera, not that orbit target.

All controls are actual named, focusable DOM actions with visible focus and current
moving/direction state. Stop, Exit, destinations, orientation and core actions
remain keyboard operable; no global WASD binding is selected. Opening any content/
help/booking panel stops travel and clears pad/continuous/route state. Leaving the
World interaction focus domain, document/application visibility loss, context loss,
release invalidation, input cancellation or Exit also stops movement. Returning,
closing a panel or restoring resources does not resume old travel automatically.
Focus transitions within the World controls are not mistaken for leaving it.
Reduced motion removes decorative camera drift/bob and animated destination travel;
an explicit stable destination change still requires current eligible readiness.

the complete compatible stable graph. Its shape-based translated movement does
