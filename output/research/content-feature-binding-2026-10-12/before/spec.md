## Problem, Audience, and Evidence

European-first English-speaking mid-market and enterprise buyers need to assess
service fit, evidence, limitations and next steps without entering the World or
using AI. Staff need to draft and review the same content for semantic pages,
department boards and approved knowledge, without presenting placeholders as
client work, available people or verified assurance.

The accepted taxonomy and reserved routes establish intended offers, not delivery
history. The protected application/seeds and campus studies establish prototype
behavior and visual direction, not publishable evidence. D-070 supplies editable
company facts; independent verification, disclosure review and contact operation
remain incomplete. This feature defines the content workflow and output criteria,
not finished public copy or a new service offer.

## User Journeys and Failure Journeys

### Primary journey

1. A visitor reaches meaningful semantic content, discovers a wing/service and
   reads its buyer decision, scope, exclusions and evidence/limitations.
2. They follow approved relevant Work, Demos, Insights, Experts or Trust links;
   unsupported entries are absent or represented by a truthful eligible empty state.
3. They may use help, Book directly or explicitly enter the World. Department
   boards link to the corresponding semantic destination and use the same release.
4. An authorized author drafts a revision with evidence/rights and proposed media.
   Independent specialist review and founder approval bind its exact snapshot.
5. The approved candidate generates all required derivatives and becomes public
   only through the accepted publication process and verified serving eligibility.

### Accessibility and non-WebGL equivalent

Complete core content and conversion are available without canvas. Initial HTML
contains meaningful headings, navigation, body and links before JavaScript.
Changed interactive processes need keyboard, touch/pointer alternatives, focus,
zoom, reduced-motion and assistive-technology verification. Required media
alternatives follow the approved media inventory; inaccessible optional media
cannot block equivalent semantic content or Book.

### Failure and recovery

Missing owner/evidence/rights or an edit after review holds the affected candidate.
Failed preparation leaves the previous eligible release available; unknown cutover
effects reconcile under the publication contract. Urgent withdrawal overrides a
visitor's old pin. Changed public context does not duplicate, cancel or confirm
private booking/handoff operations. Missing staff availability or slots use the
canonical truthful recovery paths, not static promises in a content panel.

## SEO and Publication Requirements

The accepted route inventory governs host/path/variant/status rules, not current
activation. English-only launch emits no invented translations or `hreflang`.
Each indexable page has its truthful unique title/description, canonical, visible
H1/body, breadcrumbs, ordinary internal links, robots and eligible social/media
metadata. Structured data matches visible verified facts using only the permitted
Organization, WebSite, BreadcrumbList, Service, Article, Person, ImageObject and
VideoObject types. No fabricated ratings, reviews, offers, prices or locations.

Generate sitemap/redirect/retired outputs from the active eligible release. Keep
unknown, retired, utility and query-variant rules distinct; no soft 404, unrelated
canonical, redirect loop or planning-pattern sitemap entry. Domain/webmaster
activation remains separately gated. The accepted crawler registry distinguishes
purposes and controllability; this specification selects no new current token or
production directive. Its official evidence must be refreshed before activation.

## Acceptance Scenarios

1. **Given** an approved taxonomy and held service proof, **when** staff or visitors
   inspect the service inventory, **then** formal categories remain exact and no
   reserved room, label or prototype seed is presented as delivered client work.
2. **Given** eligible semantic service content and a closed spatial wing, **when**
   a visitor browses without WebGL/JavaScript, **then** complete meaningful initial
   content and canonical help/Book paths remain available; a closed room does not
   revoke approved semantic content.
3. **Given** missing rights, expert consent, evidence or independent review,
   **when** a candidate is submitted for publication, **then** it remains held and
   cannot populate any public derivative or AI source. A missing required launch
   item is reported rather than filled with fabricated work or a person.
4. **Given** a demo and a verified case, **when** eligible representations render,
   **then** their distinct classification, actual evidence and limitations match
   visible copy, links, structured data and knowledge; no demo becomes a client result.
5. **Given** a combined-role author or a source edit after review, **when** approval
   or activation is attempted, **then** self-approval/stale approval is denied at
   the real boundary; an administrator cannot override the separation.
6. **Given** a company-fact revision, **when** its candidate is prepared, **then**
   identifiers retain their exact strings and validity retains month precision.
   Held disclosure/contact operation remains held; the live footer, schema, World
   and knowledge do not change independently before eligible publication.
7. **Given** missing derivatives, competing generations or unknown cutover effect,
   **when** publication is attempted, **then** no partial/mixed release or guessed
   completion is served. Urgent withdrawal suppresses affected old contexts and
   delayed responses; private booking/handoff truth is preserved.
8. **Given** a reserved route/pattern, draft/private item, unknown/retired URL or
   query variant, **when** actual responses and sitemap are inspected, **then** the
   exact inventory's state/status/canonical/index rules apply. No pattern becomes
   an active URL and no access restriction is replaced by robots alone.
9. **Given** fabricated schema facts, translation alternates, profiles, location,
   assurance or environmental outcome, **when** visible/metadata/knowledge outputs
   are compared with evidence, **then** unsupported publication is denied.
10. **Given** unavailable/unknown staff or refused consent, **when** a department
    offers assistance, **then** current canonical availability and transfer rules
    apply; public copy creates no live team, response promise or booking confirmation.
11. **Given** optional media lacking a required alternative or rights, **when** the
    actual semantic process is exercised, **then** affected media stays held while
    eligible content and Book remain usable through the approved accessible path.
12. **Given** the six required editorial briefs but missing qualified review or
    original value, **when** launch readiness is assessed, **then** drafts remain
    incomplete launch items and cannot be indexed as approved expert publications.
13. **Given** a complete eligible indexable page and its approved visible facts,
    **when** the actual initial HTTP response and no-JS visitor journey are
    inspected, **then** meaningful body, unique title/description, primary heading,
    canonical, robots, breadcrumbs and ordinary navigation/Book links are already
    present. Applicable author/reviewer/date, social/media and permitted structured
    data agree with the visible subject and evidence; hydration is an enhancement,
    not the only source of those essential outputs.
14. **Given** candidate output with missing initial content, mismatched metadata,
    facts absent from visible content or materially different crawler/visitor
    content, **when** the real generated responses are compared with the approved
    source and route intent, **then** the affected candidate cannot activate.
    Successful hydration, valid schema syntax or an unchanged checksum cannot
    substitute for correct initial HTML and truthful equivalent public content.
15. **Given** proposed crawler-registry entries, **when** activation is prepared,
    **then** each records exact identity/token, documented purpose, robots
    applicability, technical separability, official source/date, owner and required
    founder/legal/privacy/security approval. Stale/missing evidence or invented
    independent switches cannot establish an approved directive. Verify actual
    representative responses against the accepted policy and retain at least
    quarterly registry review. A crawler identity never grants access to private
    or draft data. Historical examples are not refreshed operational evidence;
    exact directives and external submissions remain separately held.

16. **Given** synthetic local company/team/content and other non-sensitive records, **when** local journeys or a production candidate are assessed, **then** fixtures remain recognizable as fictional and cannot establish verified work, real online staff or public claims. Real records follow the intended admin revision workflow and applicable rights/consent/review gates.
17. **Given** registration identifiers, certificate months and registered address retained privately, **when** HTML, metadata/schema, World boards or AI knowledge are prepared under D-076, **then** those deferred facts are excluded from public derivatives. An admin edit alone cannot approve later publication.
