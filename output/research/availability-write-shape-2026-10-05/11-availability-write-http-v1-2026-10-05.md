## Independent primary-source verification

Reopened the official sources on 2026-10-05 and checked the proposal's limited observations:

- [RFC 5789 §2](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc5789) describes PATCH as not inherently safe/idempotent, permits conditional application and requires atomic application of the changes. The proposal identifies durable intent as the patched resource and preserves all-or-nothing durable change/outcome; it does not present asynchronous Redis projection as part of one cross-store transaction. Its body compare and operation identity are application rules, not standardized ETag or Idempotency-Key behavior.
- [RFC 9110 §15.3.3](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9110.html#section-15.3.3) supports acceptance without completed processing or guaranteed later action. Known admission plus same-PATCH reconciliation are stricter application requirements. The RFC does not require this proposal to invent a new status route. Actual authentication challenges, method handling and conditional-request precedence still need the held transport evidence.
- [RFC 8259](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8259) identifies inconsistent duplicate-name handling, Unicode interoperability problems and the interoperable binary64 integer range. Rejection of duplicate decoded names/invalid scalars and canonical revision strings are stronger project choices, not automatic parser behavior or proof of a selected storage representation.
- [RFC 9111 §5.2.2.5](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9111.html#section-5.2.2.5) restricts compliant cache storage/reuse while explicitly limiting privacy assurances. It does not supply authorization, legal retention/deletion, screen revocation or durable outcome truth. The proposal keeps those controls separate.
- Official FastAPI [body updates](https://fastapi.tiangolo.com/tutorial/body-updates/) demonstrate typed PATCH/partial-update techniques; [response models](https://fastapi.tiangolo.com/tutorial/response-model/) demonstrate output validation/filtering; [error handling](https://fastapi.tiangolo.com/tutorial/handling-errors/) provides exception-handler overrides and includes an example exposing an invalid body. These support the limited framework observations. Tutorial dictionary merge, default/coercive models, response filtering and body/error examples do not implement this contract's current authorization, duplicate-key rejection, guarded mutation or safe redaction.
