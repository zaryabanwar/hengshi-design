## Findings and bounded conclusions

**No severity-rated correction is required for this logical proposal.**

### Catalogue identity and acyclic construction

Publication lines 467–496 identify SHA-256 of the catalogue's RFC 8785 canonical
UTF-8 bytes; each listed unit retains its separate raw-byte digest. The stored
`client/build.json` artifact must contain exactly those canonical bytes, so its
manifest byte digest can equal `client_build_sha256` without relabelling another
serialization. A source commit, hashed bundler filename or peer range is expressly
insufficient.

The catalogue is built after immutable code/registry units and before
release-specific HTML/scene assembly. It excludes its own digest, generated
catalogue/binding artifacts from the unit inventory, generated HTML/publication
bytes, release ID, scene/resource-map identities and containing manifest digest.
Compiled code cannot be rewritten to embed the digest of a catalogue that already
hashes it; mutable sidecars are prohibited. The later reviewed HTML binding can
refer to the already computed build digest, while the release manifest hashes the
resulting HTML/catalogue/scene artifacts. These are one-way identity relationships,
not a reciprocal hash definition. Exact emitted binding encoding and dependency
association still require review; this receipt proves no generated build graph.

The exact unit/import/resource coverage is an explicit compiler/registration
obligation, including transitive static and lazy-entry relationships. A Vite
manifest is treated as input evidence, not the trusted catalogue or public
envelope. Generated binding files cannot be quietly folded back into catalogue
hash inputs or omit genuine runtime dependencies from the separately validated
release plan under these rules.

### Registration, disclosure and code trust

Publication lines 498–505 and 524–540 retain private publisher-owned registration,
allowlisted immutable inputs, current authority at preparation/result acceptance,
provenance/permissions and independently verified emitted-unit agreement. A
registered hash does not approve content, waive budgets or activate a release.
Changed build/profile/decoder/configuration requires a new build/preparation
identity, consistent with the frozen private preparation binding.

Scene lines 372–380 require catalogue bytes to agree with publisher admission,
immutable HTML's reviewed public association and actual controlled client code/
registry. They explicitly reject comparison of copied digest strings as evidence.
Private preparation/job/approval records never reach the browser; only the later
reviewed closed public association does. Catalogue payloads cannot select imports,
executable URLs or an alternative executable build. The exact trusted runtime
association/enforcement is honestly held, not supplied by a checksum or metadata
match. This remains consistent with the browser-resource/security proposal.

### Required closure, lazy indexing and recovery

Publication lines 520–540 and scene lines 397–422 distinguish catalogue/descriptor/
map indexes from required resource edges. The catalogue participates in semantic
bootstrap as metadata; its indexed World code/resources do not become semantic
prerequisites. Required static resources of a requested unit belong in its complete
closure, while lazy World entry, optional detail and sound retain their individual
gates. Real HTML, static imports, preloads, service workers and cache/prefetch paths
must eventually demonstrate that distinction. No eager whole-campus/model/decoder/
audio fetch is permitted by the list of catalogue references.

Every consumer completion retains the context required by its existing boundary,
and abandoned/mismatched work is discarded before indexing/admission/attachment
while bounded work accounting persists until settlement. Build/release replacement
requires a complete eligible target and logical-destination recovery, not mixed
code/maps/old collision. Failed imports stop affected entry and expose recovery;
blind reload, tier change, mutable-current resource and decoder CDN fallback are
not remedies. Safe ground is retained only while its current context is eligible.
Private booking/handoff truth remains independent. These preserve World/loading,
